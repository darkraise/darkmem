**Production is empty, and that is what holds G-C shut.** Corroboration counts
`COUNT(DISTINCT origin_key)` and needs N = 3, or 2 when two attributable origins differ

(`human_adjudication.py:242-258` writes a `human`-kind `fact_sources` row and confirms,

corpus-vacuous today but is a live code path.

This is the same root as G-C's "under-powered on every probe" verdict of 2026-08-08. It
was read there as a property of the measurement; it is a property of the corpus.

**G-E is held shut by the corpus *and* the judge, jointly.** Articles need

*"Phase 3 stays closed and `article_synthesis` stays a placeholder"*), and they need

judge half. Only capture removes the other.

**Consequence for scope:** a version headlined "articles get a real coverage number"
would be false, because there would be nothing to score. v0.5 has two halves, and the
second is the one the gates are ultimately waiting on.

## Half A — the judge is admitted for the question it was measured on

### R1 — per-role qualification records

**The defect.** `VerificationJudge.judge()` is one gate over all four `BAR_METRICS`
(`backend/app/services/verification_judge.py:222-227`, refusal at `:135-161`, bar loop
at `:324-333`), and two consumers sit behind it that ask different questions:

- **Claim entailment for articles** (`claim_entailment.py:222`) branches on
  `verdict == ENTAILED` and nothing else (`:213` default, `:251` branch). `contradicted`
  and `unsupported` are indistinguishable to it — both are "not entailed".
- **The Tier-C contradiction scan** (`contradiction_scan.py:754`) branches only on
  `contradicted` (`:774`), and since T2.109 its verdict opens a review item: no badge, no
  rung, no place in retrieval changes.

A tree-wide search finds exactly these two `.judge()` call sites. So each consumer's gate
depends on a discrimination it never makes, **in both directions**. The failing bar —
contradiction recall — governs, for the article consumer, exactly the
contradicted↔unsupported confusion that consumer collapses.

**The argument is two steps.** "Branches only on ENTAILED" is insufficient alone: a
contradiction-recall miss can also manifest as contradicted→**entailed**, which *would*
corrupt coverage. Structurally that leak is priced by the entailed-precision bar, which
passes — but only at the pooled base rate, so a handful of contradicted-judged-entailed
pairs would barely dent 0.9884 while corrupting a fifth of the contradicted material.
The structural argument is therefore weaker than it reads, and the empirical one is what
closes it (below).

**Grounds, none of which cite the failed contradiction number.**

1. **Doc 20 §4's ratified registry already defines G-D as the entailment discrimination**
   — *"The groundedness judge's precision/recall at distinguishing grounded from
   ungrounded sentences, on a labelled gold set"*
   (`docs/design/20-roadmap-and-sequencing.md`, §4 gate table). The contradiction pair
   entered G-D through exactly one inheritance sentence in doc 17 §6 — *"The veto
   consumer inherits the same qualification on the contradiction verdict"*
   (`17-test-and-eval-harness.md:147`) — never a derivation. The split does not re-cut
   G-D; it restores G-D to its ratified charter and gives the rider its own gate.
2. **The pairing is code-forced, not chosen.** See the two consumers above.
3. **No selection was possible.** The entailment pair has never failed under any judge,
   gold, or sitting: deepseek 0.9938/0.9249 pooled and 1.0000/0.9167 Rule B (2026-08-10),
   0.9895/0.9307 (2026-08-08); gpt-5.5 0.9884/0.9827 and 0.9857/0.9583 (2026-08-12),
   0.9806/1.0000 (2026-08-08). A split cannot have been fitted to favour a metric that
   never once failed.

**Honesty checks.** Two are present-tense and can actually detect a fit:

- **The counterfactual pre-registration test.** This split is derivable from doc 20 §4's
  charter and the two call sites alone, as of 2026-08-07, before any of these numbers
  existed. If the amendment cannot be written from those two inputs without reaching for
  a measured value, it is not a derivation.
- **The miss decomposition.** In the T2.114 run, **all nine contradiction-recall misses
  were judged `unsupported`; zero were judged `entailed`** — recomputed from the per-pair
  artifact. The failing bar's entire content in that run was the distinction the article
  consumer collapses, and the contradicted→entailed leak did not occur at all. **This is
  corroborating evidence, not a ground of the amendment**: it comes from the run the
  stopping rule forbids citing, so it belongs in the record as an observation about what
  was measured, and the amendment must stand without it.

Two further tripwires remain, secondary because they are post-hoc: the split is proven a
fit if the contradiction role's bars are later relaxed, or if it is used to license
another model attempt on pair set `4975c5cc`.

**Doc 17 §6's rejected-alternatives clause must be named and distinguished, not sailed
past.** §6 rejects *"Per-consumer judges (three purposes, three models) … one
truth-critical judge, qualified once, is auditable; three drift independently"*
(`17-test-and-eval-harness.md:161`). R1 is not that: it keeps **one judge identity** and
splits the *record* by role. But roles do now hold qualification state independently,
which is the rejected clause's nearest neighbour, and an amendment that does not say so
is not an honest amendment. §6's closing sentence also binds R2: *"re-qualification after
a `verification`-purpose model swap is mandatory (the qualification result is recorded
against the model identity)."*

**What does not change.** `contradiction_recall_bar` 0.95 and `contradiction_precision_bar`
0.70 keep their values. The contradiction role stays unqualified, so the Tier-C scan keeps
dead-ending exactly as today. The deterministic veto is untouched. **The T2.114 stopping
rule stands unbreached: no model touches pair set `4975c5cc` again**, and the contradiction
role's only futures remain that rule's two named moves.

**One holding this needs that the loop cannot make.** R1 is not one of the stopping rule's
two named moves. Adopting it requires the owner to hold that it is a distinct structural
move rather than a third attempt in disguise. That holding is recorded *in* the amendment,
not assumed by it. See open questions 3 and 4.

### R2 — how the entailment record is minted

An evidence record is *"minted only by a cleared run, never by hand"* (T2.95/T2.100), so
R1 alone would strand the entailment role: there is no cleared run to mint from, and
hand-writing the JSON is what the discipline forbids.

**The mechanism:** amend `run_judge_qualification.py` to re-score a recorded per-pair
verdict artifact under a named role, minting that role's record if its bars clear — the
same offline-replay precedent the G-D resolution spike set (zero live calls, no gold
contact).

**The evidence chain is currently broken, and fixing it is part of this ruling.**
Measured 2026-08-12 in `backend/scripts/benchlib/entailment/verdicts/`:

- The directory holds **80 files. Seventy-nine are synthetic residue.**
- Seven of those carry the real gold's exact shape (173 entailed / 80 contradicted), the
  real deepseek identity `4d886440`, and real gold shas including the **current**
  `6eadba73` — one is named `2026-08-12-4d886440-6eadba73.json`, precisely how a genuine
  deepseek run on today's gold would be named. Their metrics are garbage: entailed
  precision 0.3731 against deepseek's published 0.9938.
- Files accumulate across 08-10 (25), 08-11 (20) and 08-12 (36), so this is an **active
  leak**, not one historical accident. The writer takes no override —
  `VERDICTS_DIR` is a module-level constant used directly at
  `run_judge_qualification.py:397` — so anything that drives the runner to scoring
  completion writes into the live artifact directory under real identities.
- The directory is gitignored (`.gitignore:29`) and nothing records the genuine
  artifact's checksum; the eval report pins only the gold sha.

So the mint path's input directory cannot today distinguish evidence from residue, and
*"minted only by a cleared run, never by hand"* is one text-editor session away from
being violated undetectably. **R2 therefore requires three things**: the leak fixed (its
own task); the re-score to **authenticate its input** — a candidate artifact is admissible
only if it reproduces the ledger's published metrics for that identity and gold, which
`2026-08-12-fd39e7b2-6eadba73.json` does exactly (0.9884 / 0.8875) and every stub fails by
orders of magnitude; and the minted record to **name its input's hash**.

**Only one artifact is admissible today, and open question 2 was a false choice.** The
first draft said either configured judge could be admitted because deepseek's aggregate
entailment numbers clear. They do — but **no genuine deepseek per-pair artifact exists**;
every deepseek-identity file in the directory is residue. Admitting deepseek would require
reconstructing verdicts from the quarantined cache, which the artifact's own header notes
is hampered by an identity the cache does not record. **The admissible input is `gpt-5.5`
(`fd39e7b2c47fa375`), and production's `verification` purpose must be pointed at that
identity** or `qualification_state` fails closed on mismatch and nothing changes.

**Warm-replay caveat, recorded on the record itself.** 338 of the 402 verdicts are cache
replays of the 2026-08-08 contact; only the 64 live verdicts are a genuine 2026-08-12
sitting. The record must say so where the number lives.

**Precondition — the census check nobody has run.** Rule B, the duplicate findings
(`eg-0093`/`eg-0367`, `eg-0194`/`eg-0251`) and the reproduced-retirement defect all
scrutinised the *contradicted* census. Whether the 322 entailed/unsupported pairs (173 +
149, confirmed from the artifact) carry comparable duplicate or independence defects is
**unknown**. Settled by running the same duplicate-detection pass over them, by a party
that did not author them. **No mint before that pass reports.**

### R3 — the recall derivation, corrected in the ledger

The session that proposed lowering contradiction recall to 0.90 argued that post-T2.109 a
miss costs an unopened review item rather than a wrong belief. **That is wrong**, and the
ledger's 2026-08-08 derivation is right. A recall miss is a genuinely contradicted fact
that keeps `corroborated`, keeps a badge asserting no known contradiction, and stays
citable by articles — a standing wrong belief with no backstop, since the human-verdict
arm is the scan's only route into the audit and the deterministic arm is corpus-vacuous.
A precision miss is bounded by a reviewer's dismissal. That asymmetry is doc 17 §6's rule.

**Why this is recorded rather than dropped quietly.** At a 0.90 bar the 2026-08-10
deepseek attempt clears **all four bars on both populations** (contradiction recall
0.9375 pooled / 0.9500 Rule B; the other three already passed —
`gate-ledger.md:1123-1127`). The proposed correction was not the harmless bookkeeping it
was presented as; it would have converted a recorded miss into a retroactive pass. The
ledger entry must say that, so the same derivation cannot resurface at the next gold
looking new.

One refinement while the ledger is being edited: *"the amendment is what made recall
truth-critical"* (`gate-ledger.md:1344`) is loose. The miss's cost is unchanged by T2.109;
what changed is that **precision left** the truth-critical class, leaving recall the only
truth-side metric on that arm. Same bar, cleaner reason.

**This ruling depends on nothing else in v0.5 and can land immediately.**

### R4 — populate `SINGLE_VALUED_RELATIONS`

Eight of the nine pooled recall misses are `value-swap` — *a different value in a slot
the premise fills exclusively* — and slice recall there is 0.810. That is precisely the
class the deterministic `single_valued` detector exists for, disarmed today only because
`SINGLE_VALUED_RELATIONS` is an empty frozenset (`contradiction_veto.py:114`, reasoning
comment at `:28-31`).

Populating it adds true detection capacity where the LLM judge measurably fails, using no
model, no gold, no bar and no judge — and it feeds the **absolute veto**, which is
sanctioned for the deterministic arm precisely because its precision is by construction.

**That is also the whole risk.** A relation belongs in the tuple only if the taxonomy
guarantees at most one object per subject; a wrong inclusion manufactures near-permanent
disputes on an unappealable instrument. So the deliverable is a **per-relation argument**
over `RELATIONSHIP_TYPES`, admitting only the relations that survive it, with the
rejected ones and their reasons recorded. Empty-after-analysis is a legitimate outcome.

**Honesty check:** it moves no bar and relabels nothing; it can only add detections.

**OUTCOME (T2.120, 2026-08-12): empty after analysis, and R4's premise is falsified.**
All sixteen relations in `RELATIONSHIP_TYPES` fail the at-most-one-object-per-subject
test; `SINGLE_VALUED_RELATIONS` stays `frozenset()`. The argument is recorded beside the
frozenset in `contradiction_veto.py` rather than in a doc that can drift from it.

The load-bearing part is not the rejections but *why* they cannot be worked around. The
task measured the eight value-swap misses against the taxonomy instead of reasoning about
them abstractly, and **every exclusive slot in them is an attribute** — a port, a version,
a TTL, a scrape interval, a bucket name. This taxonomy does not store attributes as
relations at all, so **the detection capacity R4 hoped for cannot be added by widening
this set, at any membership.** The one miss that maps to a stored relation (`eg-0048`,
wireguard deployed on UDP vs TCP) maps to `DEPLOYED_ON`, which is plural in ordinary use —
`postgres DEPLOYED_ON {linux, docker}` — so admitting it would manufacture disputes on the
unappealable veto to catch one pair.

**What this means for the gate.** The value-swap slice at 0.810 stays where the LLM judge
fails it; there is no deterministic instrument available to cover it today. Closing that
gap needs taxonomy-first single-valued relations minted for attribute slots (doc 06 §3.1),
which is a larger piece of work than R4 contemplated and is not scoped here. R4 delivered
a recorded negative result and one hardening test (a member must itself be in
`RELATIONSHIP_TYPES`, else the arm reads as armed while matching rows that cannot exist) —
which is the correct outcome of an argument that was allowed to come back empty.

## Half B — the corpus

Half A leaves G-C and G-E measurable and unmeasured. Closing them needs a production
corpus with more than one origin. The import route is foreclosed by the owner's greenfield
ruling (T1.53 dropped, no data carried), leaving two:

- **`T1.54` — provision the homelab instance and use it for real.** The honest route:
  origins accumulate because a person and their agents actually capture into it, across
  source-kinds and across days, which is what the corroboration rule was written to
  recognise. **Owner-gated** (`progress.md:272`, it touches the live homelab host), so the
  loop cannot start it. **It is also the version's true critical path**: the ≥ 7-day
  origin-spacing clause cannot start counting until capture starts.
- **`T3.07`/`T3.08` — the research agent with distinct-origin stamping.** Startable today,
  and it manufactures the origins rather than observing them. A corroboration count
  assembled from a loop the system ran on itself is weaker evidence than one assembled
  from independent capture.

**Recommendation: ask for `T1.54` first; take `T3.07`/`T3.08` only if the owner declines
or defers it.** And if that route is taken, **the disclosure must be machine-enforced, not
recorded in task prose** — a reserved `origin_key` prefix or a dedicated source-kind for
researched captures, so G-C's SQL can slice manufactured from observed origins and "do not
pool without saying which is which" is checkable two months later rather than remembered.

## What v0.5 claims and does not claim

**It does clear G-D as redefined, conditionally, and this must be said plainly.** Under
R1, G-D *is* the entailment pair; that pair's metrics already clear its bars on both
populations. So if `T2.118` reports clean, `T2.122` closes the leak, and the owner makes
the Q3/Q4 holdings, then `T2.119`'s mint clears the redefined G-D and opens the L2→L3
judge gate. Denying that while the task table marks `T2.119` "G-D (produces)" is the kind
of quiet claim this project's ledger exists to prevent.

It does **not**:

- clear G-D's contradiction role. That role stays unqualified against an unchanged 0.95,
  the Tier-C scan stays dead, and the stopping rule stands.
- clear G-E. It removes the judge half of G-E's blockage; the corpus half remains, and
  G-E still needs the 0.95 / 0.90 distribution bars met on live articles.
- clear G-C, which Half A does not touch at all.
- change any bar value anywhere.
