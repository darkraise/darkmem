## The measurement that shapes this version

Taken 2026-08-12 against the live production database
(`darkmem-prod-postgres-1`, image `darkraise/darkmem-api:0.4.0`, healthy), read-only:

| Table | Rows |
|---|---|
| `memories` | 2 |
| `memories`, distinct `origin_key` | **1** |
| `knowledge_triples` | 1, all `candidate` |
| `fact_sources` | 1 |
| `entities` | 2 |
| `knowledge_articles` | **0** |

**Production is empty, and that — not the judge — is what holds G-C and G-E shut.**
Corroboration counts `COUNT(DISTINCT origin_key)` and needs N = 3, or 2 when two
attributable origins differ in source-kind or lie ≥ 7 days apart. Production has one
origin. So no fact can leave `candidate`; article synthesis, which selects on
corroborated/confirmed facts, has nothing to select; and G-E's first probe — *articles
live in production* — cannot be satisfied by any amount of code.

This is the same root as G-C's "under-powered on every probe" verdict of 2026-08-08. It
was read there as a property of the measurement; it is a property of the corpus.

**Consequence for scope, stated rather than discovered later:** R1 and R2 remove a real
structural defect and make coverage *measurable in principle*. They do not produce a
coverage number, because there is nothing to score. A version headlined "articles get a
real coverage number" would be false. v0.5 therefore has two halves, and the second is
the one the gates are actually waiting on.

## Half A — the judge is admitted for the question it was measured on

### R1 — per-role qualification records

**The defect.** `VerificationJudge.judge()` is one gate over all four `BAR_METRICS`
(`backend/app/services/verification_judge.py:222-227`, `qualification_state` at
:324-335), and two consumers sit behind it that ask different questions:

- **Claim entailment for articles** (`claim_entailment.py:222`) branches on
  `verdict == ENTAILED` and nothing else (:251). `contradicted` and `unsupported` are
  indistinguishable to it — both are "not entailed".
- **The Tier-C contradiction scan** (`contradiction_scan.py:754`) branches only on
  `contradicted` (:774), and since T2.109 its verdict opens a review item: no badge, no
  rung, no place in retrieval changes.

So each consumer's gate depends on a discrimination it never makes, **in both
directions**. The failing bar — contradiction recall — governs, for the article
consumer, exactly the contradicted↔unsupported confusion that consumer collapses.

**The argument is two steps, not one, and the second is what closes it.** "Branches only
on ENTAILED" is insufficient by itself: a contradiction-recall miss can also manifest as
contradicted→**entailed**, which *would* corrupt coverage. That leak is priced by the
entailed-precision bar, which passes. The failing bar's *marginal* content for this
consumer is the collapsed distinction alone.

**Grounds, none of which cite the failed run.**

1. **Doc 20 §4's registry already defines G-D as the entailment discrimination** —
   *"the groundedness judge's precision/recall at distinguishing grounded from
   ungrounded sentences, on a labelled gold set"*
   (`docs/design/20-roadmap-and-sequencing.md:90`). The contradiction pair entered G-D
   only through doc 17 §6's *"the veto consumer inherits the same qualification"* — an
   inheritance, never a derivation. The split does not re-cut G-D; it restores G-D to its
   ratified charter and gives the rider its own gate.
2. **The pairing is code-forced, not chosen.** See the two consumers above. A
   pre-registered version of this split would have been symmetric, and this one is.
3. **No selection was possible.** The entailment pair has never failed under any judge,
   gold, or sitting: deepseek 0.9938/0.9249 pooled and 1.0000/0.9167 Rule B (2026-08-10),
   gpt-5.5 0.9884/0.9827 and 0.9857/0.9583 (2026-08-12), plus the 2026-08-08 readings. A
   split cannot have been fitted to favour a metric that never once failed.

**Honesty check.** The amendment text must be writable citing only (1) and (2) — never
the 0.8875. It is proven a fit if the contradiction role's bars are later relaxed, or if
the split is subsequently used to license another model attempt on pair set `4975c5cc`.

**What does not change.** `contradiction_recall_bar` 0.95 and `contradiction_precision_bar`
0.70 keep their values. The contradiction role stays unqualified, so the Tier-C scan keeps
dead-ending exactly as today. The deterministic veto is untouched. **The T2.114 stopping
rule stands unbreached: no model touches `4975c5cc` again**, and the contradiction role's
only futures remain that rule's two named moves.

**One ruling this needs that the loop cannot make.** R1 is not one of the stopping rule's
two named moves. Adopting it requires the owner to hold that it is a distinct structural
move rather than a third attempt in disguise. That holding is recorded as part of the
amendment, not assumed by it.

### R2 — how the entailment record is minted

An evidence record is *"minted only by a cleared run, never by hand"* (T2.95/T2.100), so
R1 alone would strand the entailment role: there is no cleared run to mint from, and
hand-writing the JSON is exactly what the discipline forbids.

**The mechanism:** amend `run_judge_qualification.py` to re-score a recorded per-pair
verdict artifact under a named role, minting that role's record if its bars clear.
`backend/scripts/benchlib/entailment/verdicts/` carries the 2026-08-12 artifact — 402
pairs with gold and judged verdicts, mechanically sufficient, **zero gold contact and
zero live calls**, the same offline-replay precedent the G-D resolution spike set.

**Identity binds.** A record is tied to a judge identity, so admitting `gpt-5.5`
(`fd39e7b2c47fa375`) for the entailment role also requires production's `verification`
purpose to carry that identity, or `qualification_state` fails closed on mismatch and
nothing changes. Deepseek's recorded verdicts also clear the entailment pair, so either
configured judge is admissible; **this spec assumes `gpt-5.5`**, since its per-pair
artifact is the one on disk. The owner should confirm — it is a cost decision as much as
a truth one, and CLAUDE.md already records that OpenAI key as a judge credential that
must never be pointed at the ingestion path.

**Warm-replay caveat, recorded on the record itself.** 338 of the 402 verdicts in that
artifact are cache replays of the 2026-08-08 contact. The run is not an independent
sitting, and the record must say so where the number lives.

**Precondition — the census check nobody has run.** Rule B, the duplicate findings
(`eg-0093`/`eg-0367`, `eg-0194`/`eg-0251`) and the reproduced-retirement defect all
scrutinised the *contradicted* census. Whether the ~322 entailed/unsupported pairs carry
comparable duplicate or independence defects is **unknown**. Settled by running the same
duplicate-detection pass over them, by a party that did not author them. **No mint before
that pass reports.**

### R3 — the recall derivation, corrected in the ledger

The session that proposed lowering contradiction recall to 0.90 argued that post-T2.109 a
miss costs an unopened review item rather than a wrong belief. **That is wrong**, and the
ledger's 2026-08-08 derivation is right. A recall miss is a genuinely contradicted fact
that keeps `corroborated`, keeps a badge asserting no known contradiction, and stays
citable by articles — a standing wrong belief with no backstop, since the human-verdict
arm is the scan's only route into the audit and the deterministic arm is corpus-vacuous.
A precision miss is bounded by a reviewer's dismissal. That asymmetry is doc 17 §6's rule.

**Why this is recorded rather than dropped quietly.** At a 0.90 bar the 2026-08-10
deepseek attempt clears **all four bars on both populations** (contradiction recall
0.9375 pooled / 0.9500 Rule B; the other three already passed —
`gate-ledger.md:1122-1127`). The proposed correction was not the harmless bookkeeping it
was presented as; it would have converted a recorded miss into a retroactive pass. The
ledger entry must say that, so the same derivation cannot resurface at the next gold
looking new.

One refinement to the ledger's own wording while it is being edited: the phrase *"the
amendment is what made recall truth-critical"* is loose. The miss's cost is unchanged by
T2.109; what changed is that **precision left** the truth-critical class, leaving recall
the only truth-side metric on that arm. Same bar, cleaner reason.

### R4 — populate `SINGLE_VALUED_RELATIONS`

Eight of the nine pooled recall misses are `value-swap` — *a different value in a slot
the premise fills exclusively* — and slice recall there is 0.810. That is precisely the
class the deterministic `single_valued` detector exists for, disarmed today only because
`SINGLE_VALUED_RELATIONS` is empty (`contradiction_veto.py:28-31`).

Populating it adds true detection capacity where the LLM judge measurably fails, using no
model, no gold, no bar and no judge — and it feeds the **absolute veto**, which is
sanctioned for the deterministic arm precisely because its precision is by construction.

**That is also the whole risk.** A relation belongs in the tuple only if the taxonomy
guarantees at most one object per subject; a wrong inclusion manufactures near-permanent
disputes on an unappealable instrument. So the deliverable is a **per-relation argument**
over `RELATIONSHIP_TYPES`, admitting only the relations that survive it, with the
rejected ones and their reasons recorded. Empty-after-analysis is a legitimate outcome.

**Honesty check:** it moves no bar and relabels nothing; it can only add detections.

## Half B — the corpus, which is what the gates are actually waiting on

Half A leaves G-C and G-E measurable and unmeasured. Closing them needs a production
corpus with more than one origin, and there are exactly two routes:

- **`T1.54` — provision the homelab instance and use it for real.** The honest route:
  origins accumulate because a person and their agents actually capture into it, across
  source-kinds and across days, which is what the corroboration rule was written to
  recognise. **Owner-gated** — `progress.md:272` marks it as touching the live homelab
  host — so the loop cannot start it. It is the first thing to ask the owner for.
- **`T3.07`/`T3.08` — the research agent with distinct-origin stamping.** Startable
  today, and it manufactures the origins rather than observing them. That distinction
  matters at the gate: a corroboration count assembled from a loop the system ran on
  itself is weaker evidence than one assembled from independent capture, and if this
  route is taken, G-C's eventual verdict owes a disclosure saying so.

**Recommendation: ask for `T1.54` first; take `T3.07`/`T3.08` only if the owner declines
or defers it**, and record the disclosure obligation with the task rather than at the
gate. Do not run both and pool the origins without saying which is which.
