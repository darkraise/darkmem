**Goal.** Give `article_synthesis` a real body: eligible subject in (T3.01),
one stored article out, synthesised from verified facts only, journalled so a
bad article is traceable to its fact set. It is the first consumer of the
`synthesis` LLM purpose, which has been seeded and inert since day one.

**What this task must not do.** It must not label anything grounded. The
entailment pass that measures coverage is `T3.03`; until it exists, no article
has a measured coverage, so no article may claim one. See D3 — the decision
most likely to be got wrong, because the status column will happily accept
`corroborated`.

## Decisions to make before coding, not during

**D1 — the exclusion is a `WHERE`, and it is doc 09 §4.2's, exactly.
(Corrected.)** The done-when says "excluded at the query, not filtered from the
draft", and the distinction is the safety property rather than a performance
note: a filter applied to a draft means the model *already saw* the disputed
fact and may have written a sentence its citation no longer supports — the
sentence survives a filter that removes facts, not prose.

The predicate is exactly:

```
verification_status IN ('corroborated', 'confirmed')
AND expired_at IS NULL
```

`disputed` and `candidate` are both `verification_status` values (baseline
CHECK), so the `IN` clause is what implements §4.2's two named exclusions.

**Revision 1 added a third line excluding facts carrying
`contradiction_suspected_at`, and called it §4.2's. It is not.** §4 names two
exclusions and mentions no suspicion flag anywhere. The mechanics revision 1
cited are real — a `corroborated` fact *can* carry the flag
(`verification_ladder.py:525`) — but implementing the extra clause would:

- silently narrow a design-stated input predicate, which this repo calls a
  behaviour change rather than a cleanup; and
- hand a **sub-bar instrument a new suppressive channel.** T2.109 measured the
  LLM contradiction scan at 0.7937 / 0.8814 / 0.9020 against a 0.95 bar and
  moved it *off* every suppressive path for exactly that reason — a flagged
  fact "keeps its rung, its badge and its place in retrieval". Excluding
  suspected facts from synthesis would re-arm it: post-G-D, a false positive
  silently drops a *true* corroborated fact from its article and, because the
  fact set is hashed (D10), flips `fact_set_hash` — so scan noise reads as
  knowledge change and drives T3.05 re-synthesis churn.

Making that exclusion would need a **Tier-3 amendment to doc 09 §4.2**
adjudicating the T2.109 tension. This plan does not make it: the corpus wins
until a human rules otherwise (runbook §5). If a later reader wants the third
exclusion, that is the escalation to write.

**D2 — the hub cap ranks by `confidence × read-heat × recency`. (Corrected
source.)** Doc 09 §4.2 fixes the formula; `K` is a `config` row defaulting to
40 (D7 says where it lives).

Read-heat is computed at job time, never a stored column (§4.1). **The
substrate is `fact_access_stats`** — the fact-keyed counter — and the pattern to
mirror is `article_eligibility.py:271–274`'s `LEFT JOIN fact_access_stats a ON
a.fact_id = v.id`. Revision 1 named `access_stats.py`, which is the
**memory-keyed** write-side feeder, owns no heat computation, and is never
touched by the eligibility scan; an implementer following it would find nothing
to reuse or would aggregate memory counters into a fact ranking.

Recency's basis is not fixed by §4.2. Use the fact's `reference_time` when
present and `created_at` otherwise — the belief window's own timestamp is what
"recent knowledge" means for a fact, and `created_at` is when darkmem heard it.
State the choice in the module docstring; if review disagrees this is a
one-line change.

**D3 — what status a synthesised article gets, before T3.03 exists.**
`candidate`, with `review_state='open'`. Not `corroborated`, and not a null
review state. *(Survived review unchanged.)*

The reasoning, because the column will accept anything: doc 09 §4.3 makes
coverage the gate — "an article below a `config` coverage threshold does not
ship as `corroborated`, it sets `review_state='open'`". An article with **no
measured coverage** is not above the threshold; it is unmeasured, which is the
same serving outcome for a different reason. Writing `corroborated` because
nothing has yet said otherwise is precisely the confident-but-wrong failure the
layer exists to prevent.

So: every article this task writes is `candidate` + `review_state='open'`, and
that is **permanent behaviour for an unmeasured article**, not scaffolding
T3.03 deletes. T3.03 adds the measurement that can raise it, and identifies
unmeasured articles by their NULL confidence.

**D4 — `confidence` stays NULL until coverage is measured.** Doc 09 §4.3:
coverage "*is* the article's stored `confidence`". No coverage, no confidence.
A default of 0.0 would read as "measured, and terrible"; NULL reads as "not
measured", which is true. The column is nullable. Assert it.

**D5 — the journal payload names the fact set, not the article's prose.**
Journal the **fact ids** in ranked order, `K`, the config values in force, the
subject key, and the model/purpose — never the draft text. The article row
holds the prose; the journal answers "which facts produced it", and ids stay
honest about facts later invalidated in a way prose would not.

Event type: **`created` with `object_type='article'`** — both already in the
closed vocabularies, so no taxonomy widening is needed. **Do not use
`consolidated`**: the dream digest groups that stream by `payload.cycle_id`,
and foreign rows would corrupt a cycle's description.

**D6 — the three inherited seams. (Seam 2 reversed.)** Three separate steps
with separate verification, because they fail differently:

- **Seam 1 (`list_open_articles` serves the raw status).** This task's, because
  this task is what first puts a row in that queue. Route it through
  `fact_badge.row_labels` and widen both enforcement scans to the `status`
  spelling — they quantify over `verification_status` and are blind to it.
- **Seam 2 (`served_status`'s `gate=`, unforwarded by `row_labels`).** **This
  task wires it, landing inert.** Revision 1 refused, reading "open on G-D's
  record" as "open now"; it means *the record is what opens it*, which is the
  T2.95 principle stated verbatim in CLAUDE.md — **"an evidence record gates
  the label, never the code."** An unconditionally-closed gate awaiting a future
  code change is exactly a code-gated label. Three authorities agree it is
  T3.02's: that principle; the fact gate's own pinned behaviour
  (`test_minting_a_record_lights_the_label_with_no_code_change`); and the
  tree's own docstring naming the task (`test_claim_serving_gates.py:180`).

  **Shape:** `verification_judge.py` — the single door, because two modules
  deciding judge qualification is the drift T2.95's scan exists to prevent —
  exposes an article `ClaimGate` derived from `qualification_state`. The
  article-serving path obtains it there and passes it through `row_labels`'s
  new `gate=` forwarding. `claim_gates.groundedness_gate()` stays as the
  unconditionally-closed fallback, so an **unwired caller fails closed**.

  **It is inert by two independent constructions**, which is why landing it is
  safe: no qualification record exists (G-D not cleared), and even with one,
  D3 writes only `candidate` and the only path that could raise an article is
  T3.03's coverage routing, itself behind G-E.
- **Seam 3 (a minted fact record must not light an article).** Keep and extend
  T2.95's test now that the article path is reachable.

**D7 — the runner is a body for the registered job. (Count corrected.)** T2.14
registered `article_synthesis` with its cron, lock and config schema. This task
replaces `PlaceholderJobRunner`.

**`article_synthesis` is the *last* placeholder, not the second-to-last.**
`DREAM_JOB_OWNERS` holds six, `SHIPPED_DREAM_JOBS` five, and `_placeholder_job`
has one call site. Revision 1 said one would remain; zero will. Two consequences
the implementer must handle rather than discover:

- `test_unshipped_family_members_are_marked_placeholders` iterates
  `DREAM_JOB_KEYS − SHIPPED_DREAM_JOBS`, which becomes **the empty set** — a
  vacuous pass. Keep it as a guard for future members and **say in its docstring
  that it is now vacuous by construction**, so the next reader does not mistake
  an empty loop for a proven property. The shipped-members test is what covers
  this swap.
- `SHIPPED_DREAM_JOBS` gains the sixth entry **including its module name**, and
  that value is load-bearing: invariant 15's source scan derives its forbidden
  module list from it, so a wrong or missing module name quietly narrows the
  hot-path guard.

The registration's *schema* does change, contrary to revision 1's "changes
nothing": the codified precedent (`topic_detection`, `contradiction_scan`) is
that a real runner replaces the placeholder's generic `batch_size` with its
real knobs, preserving persisted keys. **`K` and the length budget (D9) live
there**, on the job's `config_schema`.

**D8 — invariant 15 binds.** `article_synthesis` is a dream-job family member,
so no capture or retrieval path may reach it. The source scan already fails the
build if one does; do not add a synchronous "synthesise now" door in this task.

**D9 — the length budget is part of the article shape. (New.)** Doc 09 §4.2:
"a target length budget (a `config` word/token cap, defaulting to a few hundred
words) **with a hard ceiling**". Revision 1 omitted it entirely and took only
the `K` knob; the doc calls K and the length budget "two knobs on the same
lever". Unbounded articles become T3.23's chunking problem and inflate T3.03's
per-claim cost. The budget bounds the synthesis call (Step 2) and the ceiling is
enforced on the response, not merely requested in the prompt.

**D10 — `fact_set_hash` is over the FULL verified set, uncapped. (New.)** Doc 09
§4.4 fixes it: the hash covers "the subject's non-disputed
corroborated/confirmed facts **only**" — the whole verified set, so staleness
fires exactly when knowledge changed. Hashed over the top-K *input* set instead,
read-heat drift reorders the cap boundary and flips the hash with no knowledge
change, which is the precise failure §4.4 exists to prevent. Order-independent
digest. T3.02 writes the column T3.05 will trust.

**D11 — a re-run supersedes atomically, or skips. (New — resolves an
ambiguity.)** Revision 1 said "a re-run supersedes rather than colliding" *and*
"supersession lineage is T3.05's", which cannot both hold: satisfying
`ux_knowledge_articles_live` requires flipping the old row to `superseded`, and
doc 09 §4.4 requires `superseded_by` set with it. **`knowledge_articles` has no
`chk_superseded_has_pointer`** — unlike `memories` — so a deferred pointer does
not error, it silently loses lineage.

**Decision: Step 3 skips a subject that already holds a live article**, and all
re-synthesis (supersession, pointer, debounce, staleness) is T3.05's, whole.
That keeps this task's write path a pure insert, makes the constraint
unreachable rather than merely satisfied, and leaves T3.05 owning one coherent
mechanism instead of half of one. The skip is journalled so it is visible.

## Steps

- [ ] **Step 1 — the fact-gathering query.** New `article_synthesis.py`: given a
      subject key and project, return the ranked, capped fact set. D1's `WHERE`,
      D2's ranking and `K`. No LLM call, no writes. Tests: a disputed fact and a
      candidate fact are absent from the returned set *and* from a direct DB
      assertion on the emitted SQL; an expired fact is absent; a hub subject
      returns exactly `K`; ranking order is stable.
      **Mutation drill:** widen the `IN` clause to include `candidate` and prove
      a test reddens.
- [ ] **Step 2 — grouping, the draft call, the length budget.** Group by
      `rel_type` and `applies_when` (§4.2), build the `synthesis`-purpose
      prompt under D9's budget, parse the response into prose plus per-sentence
      candidate fact ids. Forward `seed` per the reproducibility rule — the
      basis is the canonical serialization of the **ranked fact-id set**, since
      synthesis has no single `content`. Tests against a stubbed LLM: the prompt
      carries only the gathered facts; a response citing an id not in the input
      set is refused rather than stored (the model must not invent a citation);
      a response over the hard ceiling is refused; an unparseable response fails
      the run without writing.
- [ ] **Step 3 — the article write.** One row per subject: `candidate` +
      `review_state='open'` (D3), `confidence` NULL (D4), `fact_set_hash` per
      D10, `applies_when`/`learned_in` aggregated from the supporting facts.
      **Skip a subject that already holds a live article** (D11), journalling
      the skip. Test: the second run over the same subject writes no row,
      supersedes nothing, and leaves the live article untouched.
- [ ] **Step 4 — the journal.** D5's payload, one row per run per subject.
      Test: the payload names every input fact id in ranked order and carries
      **no draft-text field and no complete sentence of the draft** (revision 1
      said "no substring", which is unsatisfiable — every character is one).
- [ ] **Step 5 — the runner.** Replace the placeholder; drive Steps 1–4 for each
      subject `select_for_synthesis` returns, bounded by `synthesis_budget`
      (D7). Apply D7's `SHIPPED_DREAM_JOBS` entry, config schema and the vacuous
      -test docstring. A per-subject failure does not abort the others and is
      recorded in **`pipeline_failures` with `pipeline_name="article_synthesis"`**
      — the `code_deriver` precedent, so a synthesis failure never reads as an
      ingest failure. Test: a budget of 2 synthesises 2 subjects and not the
      third; one subject's failure leaves the others written.
- [ ] **Step 6 — seam 1, and the queue's reason.** Route `list_open_articles`
      through `row_labels`. **Revisit its `ungated_mapping` wrap** — that
      declaration ("carries no served label") becomes false once the row's
      `status` is claim vocabulary. Widen both enforcement scans to the `status`
      spelling, **scoped to claim-vocabulary values / article-serving modules**:
      a naive widening trips on every job, buffer and memory status emission in
      `app/`. Extend `_article_reason` so an unmeasured article derives a
      coverage-unmeasured reason rather than rendering `unspecified` — every
      article D3 writes lands in that queue, so without this 100% of its article
      rows are reasonless, which is the defect class T2.112 closed.
- [ ] **Step 7 — seam 2, landing inert.** D6's shape: the article `ClaimGate`
      from `verification_judge.qualification_state`, forwarded through
      `row_labels(gate=)`, `groundedness_gate()` kept as the fail-closed
      fallback. Test: the article analogue of
      `test_minting_a_record_lights_the_label_with_no_code_change` — with no
      record the label is withheld with its reason; with a record minted in the
      test, it lights **with no code change**. Keep
      `test_the_article_gate_cannot_be_opened_by_any_record`'s intent as the
      unwired-caller case.
- [ ] **Step 8 — seam 3 + the floor.** Extend T2.95's "a minted fact record must
      not light an article" test now that articles exist — it must stay red-able
      after Step 7 wires the article gate. Run `./scripts/gate.sh`.
