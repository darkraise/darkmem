## New findings in the fix diff — all Minor, recorded not looped

**N-1 (Minor) — the restore outruns a standing, unapplied scan verdict.** The
fix report's "the veto's precedence is structural" holds for *applied*
disputes only. `contradiction_scan` journals its verdict and the **recompute**
is what writes `disputed`; between the two, the fact's status still reads
`candidate`, which is exactly what the restore's WHERE consults — it never
runs the detectors. Driven, not argued: a single-code-origin candidate fact
with a standing `contradiction_scanned` verdict that
`adjudicate_contradiction` returned **True** for at that instant was restored
to `corroborated` by `run_code_lift` (`rungs_restored=1`), then vetoed to
`disputed` by the next recompute. Graded Minor because the window is the
scan-to-recompute latency the design already sanctions for corroborated facts
(doc 08 §5.3's precision-first asymmetry, "the delay is bounded"), it
self-heals at the slow arm's next pass, the end state is identical, and G-C's
rate and audit see nothing either way (no `promote` row exists to pair the
dispute with). What the restore adds is an *upward* move during a window where
the ladder itself would refuse one. If the controller wants it closed: one
`adjudicate_contradiction` consult in `_restore_born_rungs` before restoring —
restores are rare, so the cost is a journal read almost never paid. At minimum
the fix report's precedence sentence should not be quoted unqualified.

**N-2 (Minor) — the open-belief half of the rung WHERE is unpinned.** Deleting
`AND {OPEN_BELIEF_PREDICATE}` from `_write_born_rung`

closed its window (`close_window`, the human-rejection primitive's effect),

**N-3 (Minor) — the pin-admission rule accepts a borrowed pin.** A fourth
