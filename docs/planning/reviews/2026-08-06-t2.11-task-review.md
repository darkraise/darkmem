## Verdict 2 — Task quality: **Needs fixes**

### Important

**I1 — The verdict channel has no structural writer guard, so the "one door" proof stops
one hop short of the veto.**
`contradiction_veto.py:369-377` treats `payload["contradicted"] is True` on the latest
`contradiction_scanned` row as the adjudication. The re-validation re-checks every
deterministic guard but cannot re-check the verdict itself — by design — so the single-door
property now rests entirely on `contradiction_scan.py` being the only emitter of that
event type. That is true today (grep: no other `CONTRADICTION_SCANNED` writer under
`app/`) but **unenforced**, in a codebase that builds an AST scan for every comparable
invariant (judge references, veto references, dream-job reach, RRF constants) and has no
per-event-type emitter scan anywhere. The bypass is not hypothetical: the suite's own
`_record_verdict` helper (`test_contradiction_scan.py:807-820`) plus
`test_a_journalled_contradiction_disputes_the_fact` demonstrate that one bare
`record_event` call flips a fact to `disputed` through the ladder with no judge anywhere in
the story. Done-when 2 asked for structural proof that **no other path can adjudicate**;
the scan module has that proof, the channel does not. Fix shape: an AST scan pinning
`CONTRADICTION_SCANNED` / `"contradiction_scanned"` emission to `contradiction_scan.py`
(tests may keep writing rows — the scan covers `app/`).

**I2 — A swallowed journal write is counted as completed work.**
`contradiction_scan.py:706-712` journals through `record_event`, which **never raises**
(`events.py:160-166` logs and swallows any insert failure — e.g. a missing monthly
partition, the exact substrate failure `events_ensure_partition` exists to defend). On that
path the run still increments `disputes_recorded`/`clears_recorded` and `facts_checked`,
and the watermark advances — a paid, judge-confirmed contradiction is dropped from the
veto channel while `misses` reads zero for it. The loss is delay-class (the fact is
rescanned on its next `sources_changed`), but the counters overstate completed work in
precisely the silent way clause 3 forbids, and the in-repo precedent for
journal-as-product writes is the undo module's rule: *an action that could not be
journalled did not happen — count your own rows back and raise when short*. Here the
journal row is not a side-channel; it is the job's entire output. Fix shape: verify the
row landed (count it back in the write session) and count the fact as a miss when it did
not, without advancing past it.

**I3 — A scan-opened dispute has no automated re-adjudication path, and the detector's
docstring documents a recovery that structurally cannot run.**
`detect_scanned_contradiction`'s docstring (`contradiction_veto.py:330-334`) says "a later
clean pass over the same fact is what ends a dispute the scan opened." But
`_SERVABLE_FACTS` (`contradiction_scan.py:220-229`) excludes `disputed` facts — **as doc
08 §5.3 mandates** — so once the ladder applies the dispute, no later scan pass over that
fact can ever happen; its `sources_changed` events are drained and discarded as
unservable. The documented recovery can fire only in the window before the recompute
applies the verdict (which is exactly what `test_a_later_clean_verdict_ends_the_dispute`
exercises — the fixture fact is never actually disputed). The real exits are guard-lapse
(counterpart expires, window closes, witness joins the subject) or human ratification
(§4.4). Consequence: a judge false positive over a pair whose guards legitimately keep
holding is a **permanent manufactured dispute** — the harm the precision-first asymmetry
names as the one to avoid — with no automated re-check. The exclusion itself is
plan-mandated and I do not dispute it here; what needs fixing is the docstring, and what
needs a controller ruling is whether a scan-opened dispute should ever be eligible for
re-adjudication (e.g. on a genuine provenance change) or whether human review is the
intended and only escape. Flagged as plan-mandated in part; adjudicate.
