## The bars, and why these

All six keys are `config` rows merged over committed defaults (G-D's
`VERIFICATION_BAR_DEFAULTS` shape, inherited verbatim including the raise-on-unreadable
posture). Defaults:

| Key | Default | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| `verification_false_promotion_bar` | 0.01, strict `<` | Doc 17 §5.1's own key and recommended start ("false-promotion rate < 1% of promotions per audit window"), applied to the harness's implemented quantity: DISTINCT corroborated facts later disputed or human-rejected over DISTINCT facts corroborated in window. |
| `false_promotion_min_corroborations` | 20 | Below 100 corroborations one false promotion already breaches 1%, so within the decidable band the bar demands zero observed false promotions — the same small-set granularity G-D's interim recorded (one false accept ≈ 0.944). 20 is where a zero stops being an artifact of an empty window and starts being an observation. Below it: under-powered, not evaluable. |
| `promotion_rate_floor` | 0.5 per powered bucket | Eligible rows all met their origin bar; the non-promoted remainder are veto disputes and supersession-blocked holds, which on a healthy corpus are the exception. A powered bucket where they are the majority means corpus-wide contradiction or a misfiring veto — and the veto's instrument is the judge whose interim contradiction-precision measured 0.818 against a 0.95 bar (T2.26) — so the gate holds there pending diagnosis. Stated judgement, deliberately loose: it catches a ladder that mostly refuses its own eligible facts, it does not tune the rate. |
| `promotion_rate_min_eligible` | 5 | The harness's own `UNDER_POWERED_THRESHOLD`, **imported, not re-typed**, so "powered" in the report and "decidable" at the gate cannot drift into two claims. An axis needs ≥ 1 powered bucket. |
| `promotion_rate_min_promotions` | 1 | The Done-when's "facts have actually flowed through the ladder" as a predicate: a window with zero journaled promotes cannot evidence a promotion ladder, whatever its rate arithmetic says. |
| `promotion_journal_completeness_bar` | 1.0 | Zero `knowledge_triples` rows whose `verification_status` the `verified` journal cannot explain, per the reconciliation SQL committed with the bar (`promotion_bars.JOURNAL_COMPLETENESS_SQL`). R3's born-rung carve-out is counted and reported beside the number, never absorbed. |

Promotion-rate bars are deliberately *liveness-and-honesty* bars rather than rate
targets: doc 17 §5.1 frames the rate as two-sided tuning evidence (too low argues for
loosening; false promotions veto loosening regardless), so the correctness bar is the
audit, and the rate bars exist to refuse a gate claim built on an unpowered, collapsed,
or promotion-free window.

## R1 — the citation-driven downgrade reading (required by the brief)

**Excluded from the false-promotion numerator, and the exclusion is right.** T2.48's
re-verifier marks a citation stale and the recompute *demotes* — deliberately never
`disputed`, because a deleted symbol may be a rename a human should confirm. Three
reasons, committed with the bars before any count existed:

1. The audit measures promotion **precision** — was the fact wrong when promoted. A
   stale citation is evidence *withdrawal* under world change, not a finding of falsity;
   the system's own refusal to write `disputed` says so.
2. Counting demotions would couple the false-promotion rate to repository churn: every
   refactor of an indexed repo would raise the one number that vetoes threshold
   loosening, for reasons unrelated to promotion correctness.
3. Nothing is lost in the unsafe direction: the badge drops immediately on the demote,
   and a claim later found actually false enters the numerator through the normal doors.

Boundary case, stated and bounded: a fact promoted on a citation **already stale at
promotion time** (staleness detection lags reindex by one `citation_reverify` tick)
appears only as a demote and is invisible to the audit; exposure is one reverify cron
period, after which `LIVE_SOURCE_PREDICATE` stops the stale row from counting toward any
future promotion.

## T2.101 verified in shipped code, and the blind spot it opens (R2)

Verified, not trusted: `entity_merge._fold_open_facts`
(`backend/app/services/entity_merge.py`) writes **no** verification column (keeper chosen
by rung precedence, so the surviving rung is the one the sanctioned door already wrote)
and journals **no** `verified` row — the module's only `record_event` is the `merged` row;
the retired duplicate goes through `expire()`, which journals `expired`, a different event
type outside every G-C metric. A fold cannot land in the promotion numerator. **But the
fold can move a journaled corroboration's identity out from under a later dispute**: the
audit joins on `object_id`, and when the keeper's `corroborated` rung was never journaled
(a born-corroborated row) while the retired row's promotion was, a later dispute lands on
the keeper's id where no corroboration event exists — the pair never joins and a genuine
false promotion goes uncounted. Direction: the audit **undercounts** — anti-conservative,
the one direction this metric must not err in silently. Mitigation, committed with the
bars: every measurement reports the in-window fold count beside the audit (this window:
**0** `expired` fact events on both targets), and a non-zero count caps what the audit's
zero can claim.

## The reason the gate should not clear — looked for hardest, and found

1. **Starvation is structural, not circumstantial.** No journal anywhere this machine
   can see has ever recorded a fact exiting `candidate`. Production is empty; the only
   corpus is 100% anonymous-origin and cannot meet any N. There is no honest number to
   clear the gate with, and no amount of re-running changes that.
2. **The false-promotion audit cannot produce a non-vacuous number until the
   `verification` judge qualifies.** Machine disputes flow only through
   `contradiction_scan` → `VerificationJudge.judge()`, which refuses unconditionally
   without a qualification record (minted only by T2.41 after T2.54's gold); human
   rejections flow only through a review queue that only disputes and blocked holds can
   fill. Both numerator arms dead-end today. **G-C has G-D's judge qualification on its
   critical path** — a dependency stated nowhere before this run (T2.40's Depends list
   does not carry it). Even a perfectly corroborating corpus measured today would yield
   an audit zero that measures *inoperability*, not precision — and clearing the gate on
   it would certify the ladder using an audit that could not have caught a failure.
3. **Once the record is minted, the badge outruns the measurement.** Born-corroborated
   rows (birth *and* the post-dispute restore — both through `_write_born_rung`,
   verified: no `record_event` on either path) enter neither numerator nor denominator
   of anything this gate measures, and the record this gate mints will light their
   badges on doc 10 §6's argument alone. Stated in the ledger where the number lives,
   as the task requires; a future cleared G-C row must be read that narrowly.
