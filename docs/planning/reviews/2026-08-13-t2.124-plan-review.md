## O1 — the reviewer's read: the default is wrong, C = 80

The plan defaults to C = 40 on the grounds that no score-free argument outweighs the
mechanical rule. **One exists, and it was available before any judge ran:**
discrimination at a fixed bar is a property of N and the bar alone. At the bar
boundary a judge whose true contradiction recall is 0.90 clears 38/40 with probability
≈ 0.22, against ≈ 0.09 at 76/80 (≈ 0.14 at 57/60) — halving the census more than
doubles the false-clear probability for near-bar judges, computed from the binomial
with no measured result cited. Since this gold is the surface a future
contradiction-role re-attempt sits on, C = 40 quietly converts an unmoved 0.95 bar
into a materially weaker instrument. C = 80 also carries T2.110's ratified-size
precedent and fits the resample's full-census contradicted stratum inside n = 93.
