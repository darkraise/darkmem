## Job 2 — new breakage introduced by v2

### C-v2-1 (Critical) — the sizing constant 0.985 is mis-attributed; the committed artifacts refute "2-claim shortfall, unexplained"; the route misses 490 in expectation — the third sizing error

§2: *"The two promoting groups hold 133 claims and the audit counts 131 corroborations —
a standing 2-claim shortfall at today's size, unexplained and not introduced by this task.
Sizing therefore uses the realized conversion 131 ⁄ 133 = 0.985."*

The committed unsimulated run artifact
(`docs/eval/2026-08-14-t2.129-unsimulated/bench-run-nosim.txt`) decomposes the 131
exactly, and v1's I1 had already published the decomposition v2 needed:

- `gate_fired=diversity`: **98** promote events over 98 distinct facts (line 23);
- `gate_fired=none`: **33** promote events (line 24), of which **32** are the `none`
  group's 32 claims (census line 36: `ledger corroborated origins=3 → 32 facts`) and **1**
  is the armed subject's corroborated-then-disputed fact (line 47,
  `corroborated_first=True`) — a fact in the *arming* group, which holds none of the 133
  promoting claims.

So the promoting groups realize **130 of 133**, not 131 of 133. The shortfall is **3, not
2**, it is **entirely in the diversity arm (98⁄101 = 0.9703; `none` is 32⁄32 = 1.000)**,
and the 131st corroboration comes from a group growth adds zero members to. The blended
0.985 credits the diversity arm with the `none` arm's perfection plus the armed fact's
free corroboration — the same genus of numerator/denominator mis-attribution as I1, made
while correcting I1, with I1's own decomposition on the page it was answering.

**"Unexplained" is also refuted at the accounting level, and the mechanism is visible in
the census.** Project `default` holds 98 corroborated facts at `origins=2 kinds=2` (the
design working), **14 one-origin candidates** (line 30), and **4 born-corroborated
code-only facts** (line 31; `code lift: facts_created: 4, sources_added: 102`, line 10).
The fixture asserts 102 relations (101 imports + 1 language edge, line 9): 98 imports
seconded a memory-lifted fact, and the code halves of the **3 claims whose memory-side
lift did not converge on the code assertion's canonicalised triple** were born as
code-only facts beside the language fact (98 + 4 = 102 sources — the arithmetic closes).
The failure is extraction-side (a divergent object/rel emission or a dropped triple on 3
specific contents), clock-independent (the same 98 / 14 census appears in pass 1, pass 2
and the unsimulated run), and — because all three runs replayed one cached extraction
(`cache_unknown=246`, 0 live calls) — vouched for by a single live run, so the true
conversion uncertainty for *new* names is wider than the deterministic replay suggests.
It is also name-dependent, and growth reaches for ~255 progressively more obscure
dependencies to stay plausible, so there is no basis for assuming it scales linearly —
the honest prior is that it scales the same or worse.

**Consequences, each verified:**

1. **The route misses its own Done-when in expectation.** At the measured per-arm rates,
   the 70% split expects 255 × 0.9703 + 109 × 1.000 = 356.4 new corroborations →
   131 + 356.4 ≈ **487.4 < 490**. Even on v2's own blended constant it is short:
   364 × 0.985 = 358.5 → 489.5 < 490 (⌈359 ⁄ 0.985⌉ = 365, not 364 — the derivation
   carries an off-by-one before the deeper error). The Done-when reads "≥ 490", so this
   is a designed coin-flip-or-worse on the task's first checkbox, discovered at Step 3
   after every capture is authored and the live-run budget spent.
2. **Step 3's reconciliation instrument is mis-calibrated in both directions.** "Authored
   promoting claims × 0.985 ≈ counted corroborations, per project": in `default` the true
   factor is ~0.970, so the blended check expects ~5 more corroborations than the design
   can deliver at the recommended split — a tolerance wide enough to swallow a real
   supersession loss of 3–4; in the `none` projects the true factor is exactly 1.000, so
   0.985 grants ~1.6 claims of slack per 109 that the design says must not exist. The
   plan's principal run-time catch is blunted precisely where §7 relies on it.
3. **The fix is fully determined and cheap.** Size per arm — diversity at ≤ 0.97, `none`
   at 1.00 — add an explicit claim margin (~+10 claims; ≈ 372–375 total new claims,
   ≈ 595–610 captures at the 70% split), state a top-up protocol (a Step-3 shortfall is
   repaired by authoring extra `none` claims — mechanical, no fixture change), and split
   Step 3's factors per arm. Optionally: identify the 3 failed claims (one SQL against
   the retained bench database or the next run's census) before trusting any
   extrapolation — if the mechanism is alias-prone package names, growth can avoid the
   class and beat 0.97; and re-wording those 3 committed contents is a legitimate,
   disclosable design repair worth +3.

## Job 3 — the two adjudications

Autonomous-loop §4 makes this reviewer the escalation target and the answer the decision.
Neither call is owner-only: (a) is fixture-design plausibility with several defensible
answers, exactly the class §4 routes here; (b) is task-text interpretation the controller
already holds Tier-1 amendment authority over, with no bar, gate or scored rule touched.
Both are decided below. The owner may of course override either; nothing here requires it.

### (a) The plausible-repo ceiling — DECIDED

**Ceiling: six repos total (the existing `payments-gateway` plus at most five new), each
new repo carrying 40–90 distinct third-party imports, none exceeding the existing repo's
101; the recommended shape is 4–5 new repos averaging ~65–75 imports.** That yields
~260–340 claimable (repo, dependency) pairs against the ~260–265 diversity claims the
corrected sizing needs at the 70% split — feasible without strain, with the margin coming
from `none` claims rather than a seventh repo.

Three properties beyond the counts, because manufactured-ness is a shape, not a number:

1. **Overlap is required, not forbidden.** Under the per-repo widening granted in (b),
   sibling repos may — and in aggregate must — share common dependencies. A fleet of
   Python services with pairwise-disjoint dependency sets is itself the manufactured
   signature: every real fleet shares its httpx/pydantic/sqlalchemy core. (Under the
   letter reading this property is unsatisfiable, which is one more way the letter reading
   closes the route rather than shrinking it.)
2. **Each new repo keeps ≥ ~10% of its imports unclaimed.** The existing fixture claims
   101 of 101 — a claim manifest wearing a repo's clothes. Imports are free fixture rows;
   claims are what cost captures. Repos whose import sets exist only to be claimed read as
   instruments.
3. **Role coherence.** Each repo's dependency set must belong to its stated service (a
   notification service imports twilio and sendgrid, not pyarrow), and the fleet stays one
   stand-in org, consistent with `corpus.json`'s existing world (default/ledger/atlas).

**If the adjudicated split ever needs more pairs than this ceiling yields, the split moves
toward `none`; the ceiling does not move.** Under a letter ruling on (b) this adjudication
is moot: the existing repo is fully claimed (101 claims ↔ 101 imports, bijective under the
committed validator), so the diversity arm is closed to meaningful growth and the route is
`none`-heavy at ≈ 3× the captures.

### (b) The Done-when validator conflict — INTENT, GRANTED WITH CONDITIONS

**Ruling: the per-repo form of `validate_diversity_group_against_fixture` preserves the
invariant's substance and is admissible; the widening is granted, conditioned on the tests
below.** The Done-when's "no validator weakened" clause is satisfied by demonstrating —
not asserting — that the delta is exactly the adjudicated widening.

The disclosure was weighed: the controller wrote both the clause and the plan that needs
it widened, and prefers the widening. The grant therefore rests on code this review read
independently, not on the plan's framing. The invariant the letter protects is
claim-to-fact bijectivity — two admissible claims must not converge on one fact, or the
claim census overstates the corroboration census (`promotion_corpus.py:851-925`, the
docstring's own rationale). Under per-repo subjects that bijectivity survives at the fact
layer, structurally: `triple_key` embeds the subject, so "repo-A DEPENDS_ON X" and
"repo-B DEPENDS_ON X" are distinct facts; the code lift's candidate match joins on the
canonicalised **subject name** within `CODE_FACT_PROJECT`
(`fact_lift.py:727-758`), so each repo's parse can second only the fact naming that repo;
and each code origin is per-repo (`source_ref = rp.id`), so the two facts' second origins
are distinct instruments. Cross-repo seconding is not merely unlikely — it has no code
path. The widening admits only pairs that are genuinely distinct facts and continues to
refuse every pair the old validator rightly refused.

The letter reading's cost is stated so the choice is visible: it closes the multi-repo
route entirely (the existing repo is claim-saturated), forcing an all-`none` route of
≈ +1092 captures (5.4×) — 1.9× the cost, buying no independence the diversity arm lacks,
since both arms are authored by the same party either way. That cost is not the reason for
the grant — substance-preservation is — but it confirms the letter buys nothing the
invariant needs.

**v2's two proposed properties are necessary and NOT sufficient.** Keep both; add three:

1. **The widening-delta pair test.** One test asserting, side by side, that two claims
   naming the same dependency in *different* repos are admitted AND two claims naming the
   same dependency in the *same* repo are still refused. This is what makes "no validator
   weakened beyond the adjudicated widening" a demonstrated property of the diff rather
   than prose.
2. **Subject binding, enforced not assumed.** Every diversity claim's content must name
   **exactly one** fixture repo — zero is refused, not just cross-repo ambiguity — and the
   named repo must itself import the claimed dependency (binding validity). v2's property
   1 states the shape; the validator must enforce it with the same word-boundary rigor as
   the existing dependency match, because subject binding is the entire mechanism by which
   the facts are distinct: a claim relying on dependency uniqueness alone, with no repo
   named, leaves the extraction's subject unpinned and re-opens the silent one-origin
   hold. The loader must also refuse duplicate repo names/ids across fixtures — identical
   subjects would collapse the distinctness the grant rests on.
3. **Per-repo reconciliation at Step 3.** All diversity claims live in `default`, so the
   per-project check cannot see a per-repo miss. Step 3 must assert counted
   corroborations per repo against authored claims per repo at the diversity arm's own
   conversion factor.

## Overall verdict — REWORK, narrowly scoped

v2 is a genuine correction round: both Criticals are properly withdrawn, the rewritten
mechanisms are accurate against the code, and the target-margin logic is sound. It is
returned anyway, for the reason the brief anticipated: the failure mode of believing a
number the artifacts refute did not exhaust itself in one round. C-v2-1 sits in the

embed a constant the committed census decomposes differently — and a plan that authorizes
~600 captures may not carry a sizing that misses its own floor in expectation for the
third time.

The rework is bounded, and this review pins every input to it: the per-arm constants
(0.9703 / 1.000) and their provenance, the corrected claim count and margin (~372–375
claims, ≈ 595–610 captures at 70%), the Step-3 per-arm/per-repo reconciliation, the ledger
paragraph (I-v2-1), the digit fixes (M-v2-1), the task markers (M-v2-2), and both Step-0
adjudications with their conditions. v3 is a transcription. Provided v3 contains exactly
these changes and nothing else, its re-review may be a delta check against this document
rather than a fourth full round; any new mechanism claim or any departure from the
adjudicated conditions re-opens a full review.
