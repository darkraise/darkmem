## Critical

### C1 — The qualification's independence is misstated on every canonical surface, and the branch's own "fourth model" requirement is contradicted without reconciliation

Three facts, each individually recorded somewhere on the branch, are jointly absent from
the ledger entry, the eval report, and the minted record — the three surfaces a future
reader will actually consult:

- **The judge model contacted 114 of the 280 measured pairs before the run.** gpt-5.5 sat
  the 2026-08-16 resample draw (93 pairs, `resample_runs/2026-08-16-gpt-5-5-cac8c974.json`)
  and the 29-pair bare-sortal probe (`probe_runs/2026-08-16-bare-sortal-gpt-5-5.json`);
  the union is 118 pairs, 114 still live in the measured gold. The ledger entry
  (`gate-ledger.md:2433-2435`) and report (`docs/eval/2026-08-17-judge-qualification-fd39e7b2.md:27`)
  say categorically "none was contacted before this attempt". That is true only under
  Rule B's term of art (qualification-attempt contacts counted by the run ledger); as
  plain prose beside an independence claim it is false, and the same branch records the
  contact and even the objection (ffae39fb: "gpt-5.5 has now seen 93 of this gold's
  pairs and carries the resample-circularity objection as a T2.125 judge").
- **Every pair gpt-5.5 was known to disagree with was retired before the run.** Re-scoring
  its sitting and probe against the final gold: its sole sitting disagreement (eg-0486)
  and both remaining probe rejections (eg-0475, eg-0699) are retired; every pair it
  accepted stayed live with its label. After the repairs, zero surviving pairs carried a
  known gpt-5.5 disagreement. Mitigations are real — the retirements route through
  owner-ruled clauses argued linguistically (f8db4055), retire-and-replace never flips a
  label, eg-0465 was retired *against* gpt-5.5's accepting verdict, and eleven pairs
  v4-pro rejected stayed live — but the correlation is exactly the shape the round's own
  grounds condemn, and no canonical record discusses it.
- **The step-14 record still requires what the run then violated.**
  `gate-ledger.md:2406-2412` and the round record's U1 both state: "T2.125 needs a
  fourth model — deepseek, gpt-5.5 and glm-5.2 are all now spent against this lineage."
  T2.125 ran gpt-5.5. The owner's licence exists only in commit messages (ffae39fb "the
  owner accepted this before the run"; 60953e70 "the trade the owner stated in front of
  the choice"). A ledger that both demands a fourth model and records a qualification on
  the third, with no reconciliation, cannot support the mint as written.

**Why this is Critical and what it is not.** The concrete failure is record-level: an
auditor either wrongly trusts the "none contacted" independence claim or wrongly concludes
the mint was unlicensed. It is *not* a numerical failure — the pass is robust to the
worst-case counterfactual: restoring the three known-rejected pairs as live entailed and
assuming the judge misses all three gives recall 102/110 = 0.927 (bar 0.90) and precision
102/103 = 0.990 (bar 0.95); contradicted→entailed stays 0; and the qualification prompt
demonstrably does not inherit the resample excerpt's behaviour (with the rulebook in front
of it gpt-5.5 read 80/80 contradicted correctly; at qualification, 63/80). **Fix shape:**
one ledger entry reconciling the fourth-model requirement with the owner's recorded trade,
carrying the 114-pair prior-contact disclosure and the retired-disagreement analysis; a
correction note on the report's Rule-B paragraph. No re-run is compelled by the evidence;
whether the owner wants one on a fourth model anyway is the owner's call, made with this
in view.
