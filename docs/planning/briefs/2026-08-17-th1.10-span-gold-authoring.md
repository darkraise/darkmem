## What the gold is for

`TH1.10` qualifies the verification judge for the **faithfulness role** — a
third role beside `entailment` and `contradiction`, and its own role on purpose:
the discrimination is the same word ("is this claim entailed?") over a different
premise distribution. Article claims are put against L2 fact text; faithfulness
claims are put against **raw document span bytes**, which the claim/fact gold
does not represent. So the entailment record qualifies articles and not this,
and a separate gold is what makes the separation real rather than asserted.

The metric being qualified is **span-support coverage** = entailed answer-claims
÷ total answer-claims, per answer. Three properties of that metric decide what
the gold must contain:

- **The claim unit is the answer sentence.** No sub-sentence splitting.
- **The premise is the cited span set, verbatim** — the ordered concatenation of
  exactly those span bytes. The judge sees no surrounding block, no document
  fallback, no search-for-support.
- **Whole-hypothesis scoring.** A conjunction with one unsupported conjunct is
  not entailed. Partial credit does not exist.

| Thing | Path |

raises `SpanGoldAbsent` rather than scoring an empty set as a pass, so the file

## The exact shape the loader accepts

`load_span_gold` refuses a document with no `pairs` key, a pair with no usable
`id`, a duplicate `id`, and a pair missing `answer_id` or `hypothesis`.
`validate_pair_shape` refuses a bad verdict, a bad provenance, a reserved
provenance, and an empty `premise_spans`. One pair:

```json
{
  "id": "sg-0001",
  "answer_id": "ans-mvcc-01",
  "hypothesis": "Postgres uses MVCC for concurrency control.",
  "verdict": "entailed",
  "provenance": "hand-authored-fixture-span",
  "slice_tags": ["verbatim"],
  "rationale": "General procedure 1: the span asserts the claim in the same words.",
  "premise_spans": [
    {
      "fixture_path": "span_docs/doc_mvcc.txt",
      "content_hash": "<sha256 of the fixture file's utf-8 bytes>",
      "block_id": "b1",
      "char_span": [0, 44],
      "text": "Postgres uses MVCC for concurrency control."
    }
  ]
}
```

Four things the loader enforces that are easy to get wrong:

1. **`verdict` is binary — `entailed` or `not-entailed`.** There is no
   `contradicted` here, deliberately: no consumer makes a contradiction
   discrimination over spans, and a `contradicted` census would mint numbers no
   role's gate reads. Hard negatives (value swaps, reversals) are still
   authored; they are labelled `not-entailed` and carried by their slice.
2. **`provenance` must be `hand-authored-fixture-span`.** The other two values
   (`document-span`, `qa-draft`) exist in the vocabulary but are **reserved at
   zero** until their producer tasks exist, and the loader refuses a pair
   claiming one. They are placeholders for later rounds, not options now.
3. **`premise_spans` is an ordered list**, not a string. A pair may cite several
   spans; the premise is their concatenation joined by a blank line, in the order
   given. This is the whole reason the claim/fact schema could not be reused.
4. **`content_hash` byte-binds the anchor.** `resolve_anchor_text` re-reads the
   fixture, re-hashes it, and raises `SpanAnchorDrift` on any mismatch — so
   editing a fixture document after authoring a pair against it fails loudly and
   forces retire-and-replace, exactly as corpus drift does for `l2-fact`
   premises. **Do not type hashes or offsets by hand.** Write them with a script
   that locates the substring in the file and hashes the actual bytes; the
   existing fixture builder
   (`backend/scripts/benchlib/tests/fixtures/faithfulness_gate_fixture.py`)
   shows the three-line pattern and exists precisely so a hand-typed offset can
   never silently disagree with the file it claims to anchor.

`char_span` bounds are checked against the hash-matching document. An empty range
(`end == start`) is deliberately *not* refused — that is a known separate defect
in `_span_literal`'s storage (TH1.04) and is yours to fix, not the loader's.

## Composition

- **≥ 200 pairs** (doc 17 §6), **near-misses the majority.** A gold of easy pairs
  measures nothing: the judge's job is the boundary, so the boundary is where the
  pairs must sit.
- **One slice per pair**, carried in `slice_tags` (a **list** of strings, even
  when it holds one), and a `rationale` citing a named rule from your
  `LABELLING.md` — not a free-text justification.

  > **Watch this field — nothing validates it.** `load_span_gold` and
  > `validate_pair_shape` check the verdict, the provenance, the ids and the
  > spans, and check `slice_tags` not at all. `score_span_judgments` reads
  > `pair.get("slice_tags", [])`, so a **typo'd tag silently creates a phantom
  > slice of one**, and a pair with the field **missing entirely scores into the
  > aggregate and into no slice at all** — invisible in a report whose whole
  > discipline (ledger rule 4) is that per-slice numbers are never collapsed.
  > Pin your slice vocabulary in `LABELLING.md` and check the emitted per-slice
  > counts sum to the pair count before certifying. Recorded as a gap in
  > `TH1.17`'s loader rather than fixed here.
- **Four span-specific slices** the claim/fact gold has no analogue for, and they
  are the point of this gold rather than decoration:
  - `span-truncation` — the citation cuts off before (or starts after) the clause
    that would support the claim. The surrounding block supports it; the citation
    does not. Verdict `not-entailed`. This is the failure a span-anchored
    citation system uniquely invents.
  - `context-dependence` — the span supports the claim only through an antecedent
    outside it ("it", "this approach", a table cell without its header). Verdict
    `not-entailed`, by the judge-sees-only-the-spans rule. The slice exists to
    catch a judge that resolves anaphora from world plausibility.
  - `multi-span-composition` — the claim is entailed only by the conjunction of
    two or more cited spans, no single one sufficing. Verdict `entailed`. The
    recall-side hard case for a judge that reads only the first span.
  - `structure-fragment` — the span is a list item, table fragment, or
    heading-scoped line whose meaning depends on document structure the span text
    carries only partially. Mixed verdicts, decided by what the bytes alone
    assert.
- **Plus the transferable slices**, reinterpreted over document prose: verbatim,
  paraphrase, weakening, overreach, value-swap, unrelated.
- **The claim/fact temporal slice does not transfer** — block spans carry no
  validity windows. Temporal material appears only as ordinary prose content.

## The procedure, in the order it must happen

1. **Write `LABELLING.md` first, rule-then-pairs.** The rules exist before any
   pair does. This is not ceremony: T2.124 spent most of its length discovering
   that a rule written to settle a pair already in front of you is a fitted rule,
   and the ordering is what stops that. Reuse the claim/fact gold's discipline
   wholesale — append-only with declared counts, retire-in-place never delete,
   provenance vocabularies pinned at zero by test, proposition-level (not
   byte-level) independence.
2. **Author the fixture documents.** Real prose, several hundred words each,
   varied structure — you need headings, a list and a table for
   `structure-fragment` to be authorable at all. They are committed and hashed;
   once a pair anchors into one, editing it retires the pair.
3. **Author the pairs**, blind to any judge output.
4. **Two independent blind labelling passes**, with owner escalation on
   disagreement. Judge output (`cache_span/`, any `verdicts/` sibling) is
   quarantined from every author and adjudicator.
5. **Then, and only then, the measurement.** `TH1.10` runs it once.

## Two contamination warnings, both learned the expensive way

**CLAUDE.md auto-injects into every agent's context, and it carries measured
judge scores.** T2.124 disclosed this: every blind author and labeller on that
round reported it. CLAUDE.md states the four contradiction-recall figures and the
bar values verbatim, so any agent you use for *any* part of this — even
mechanical work like computing hashes — is not blind in the sense the protocol
means. Keep agents off the authoring and labelling passes, or state precisely
what each one saw.

**Do not look at a faithfulness number before the gold is certified.** The bars
are already committed (below) and were derived, not fitted, before any build. The
value of that ordering is destroyed by one glance at a result followed by one
edit to a pair.

## The bars this will be scored against — already committed, not yours to set now

In `backend/app/services/faithfulness_bars.py`, landed by TH1.01 before any of
this existed:

| Bar | Value | What it gates |
|---|---|---|
| `faithfulness_gate_precision_bar` | 0.95 | the faithfulness role's qualification, truth-critical side |
| `faithfulness_gate_recall_bar` | 0.90 | the same qualification, other side |
| `faithfulness_coverage_bar` | 1.0 | the per-answer serving boundary — the grounded label asserts *full* grounding, so it is length-invariant by design |

All three are **floors** under the ladder's clamp shape, with a per-parameter
conservative direction and a `faithfulness_bar_licences` row. Tightening is free;
loosening needs a recorded argument, and none may move to make a run pass.

## How to check a draft without spending anything

```
cd backend && python scripts/benchlib/run_faithfulness_gate.py
```

With no `--live` this loads and shape-validates the gold, reports the pair count
and prints the committed/effective/status line for every bar. It makes **no model
call** — scoring is opt-in behind `--live` *and* `--api-key`, and the runner
fails closed rather than probing when either is missing. Run it after every
authoring session; it will catch a duplicate id, a drifted hash, a missing
`answer_id` or `hypothesis`, a bad verdict or provenance, and an out-of-bounds
span immediately, and none of that costs an attempt.

Two things it will **not** catch: a wrong or missing `slice_tags` value (above),
and whether a label is *right*. The second is what the two blind passes are for.

Note the runner also reports, in its own words, that it does not check for a
qualification record — so a present, scored gold clearing its bars is not yet the
same as a licensed serving decision. `TH1.10` is what turns one into the other.
