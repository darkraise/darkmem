## The three changes

**1. The absolute veto stands on the deterministic arm alone.**
`contradiction_veto.CONTRADICTION_DETECTORS` currently holds
`(detect_open_contradiction, detect_scanned_contradiction)`. The scanned detector leaves
that tuple. `adjudicate_contradiction_finding` then adjudicates only what is precise by
construction. No data migration: the LLM arm never fired in production (dark behind the
unqualified judge), so no existing `disputed` fact needs re-adjudication — verify that
claim rather than trusting it.

**2. The scan's finding opens review instead of disputing.**
`verification_ladder.REVIEW_OPENERS` gains a fourth predicate (doc 08 §6,
contradiction-suspected). Note `derive_review_state`'s own docstring: *"Adding an opener
is appending a predicate here, not adding a writer"* — the derivation is total and the
clear is the absence of every opener. The signal has to reach `decide()`, which today
takes `open_contradiction`; a suspected contradiction must **not** enter that parameter or
it disputes. The fact keeps its rung and its badge while flagged. A human confirming the
flag disputes through the already-live `human_adjudication.reject_fact` door — do not
build a second one.

**3. The metric pair inverts, and the new bar fails closed.**
`verification_judge.BAR_METRICS` gains a `contradiction_recall_bar → contradiction_recall`
entry. **`contradiction_recall_bar` gets no default in `VERIFICATION_BAR_DEFAULTS`**: with
no config row, `get_verification_bars` must fail closed and `qualification_state` must
report unqualified, naming the missing bar. This is deliberate — both measured judges'
numbers are public, so a default chosen now is a bar chosen while looking at them. Read
the existing malformed-bar-row test: it already establishes that this module fails loud
rather than silently defaulting, and your change must match that posture, not invent a
second one.

## Guard the thing that caused this

Add a test that fails the build if the gate can qualify a judge which misses most
contradictions. The concrete case, verified against the shipped scorer on 2026-08-08: a
judge emitting `contradicted` on exactly one correct pair and `unsupported` elsewhere
scores contradiction-precision 1.0 and **passes all three currently-gated bars at
contradiction-recall 0.0182**. Under the new `BAR_METRICS` that must be impossible to
qualify. Prove the guard reddens by mutation (drop the recall key and watch it fail),
because a guard that cannot fail is this epic's characteristic defect.

## What must not change

The `disputed` rung itself, the conjunctive recovery, the demotion hysteresis,
`_write_born_rung`, human-only `confirmed`, and `record_event`'s never-raises contract.
Invariant 15 (no dream-family member reachable from capture or retrieval) and the closed
taxonomies still bind. `CLAUDE.md` is contract — update the sections your change makes
stale, in the same commit, and do not leave a "here is the full list" sentence wrong.
