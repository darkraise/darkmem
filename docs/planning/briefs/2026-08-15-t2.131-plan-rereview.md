## Job 3 — the two adjudications

Autonomous-loop §4 makes Fable the escalation target for a design call with more
than one defensible answer, and **your answer is the decision** — unless you rule
the call owner-only, which you must say explicitly if so.

**(a) The plausible-repo ceiling.** §3.1 says one stand-in repo cannot carry 465
distinct dependencies without becoming implausible, and plausibility is the
premise the corpus rests on. So: **how many stand-in service repos, at what
dependency count each, before the fixture set is itself manufactured?** This
number sets §4's split and therefore the whole cost. v2 deliberately leaves it
unset.

**(b) The Done-when validator conflict.** Multi-repo support requires making
`validate_diversity_group_against_fixture` per-repo. That is a modification and,
in letter, a **widening**: two claims naming one dependency become admissible
when seconded by different repos. T2.131's Done-when says "every existing
validator passes unmodified and the diff shows no validator weakened".

- Read as **letter**: the multi-repo route is closed, diversity is capped at the
  plausible ceiling of the single existing repo, and the sizing changes again —
  probably toward the `none`-heavy end, at roughly 3× the captures.
- Read as **intent**: the invariant's substance is "two claims must not converge
  on one fact", and distinct repo subjects make them distinct facts, so the
  per-repo variant preserves it and the widening is admissible.

**Disclosure you must weigh rather than take on trust: the controller wrote both
the Done-when and the plan that needs it widened, and prefers the widening.** Do
not grant it on the controller's framing. If the intent reading holds, say what
property must be tested to keep it honest — v2 offers one-repo-binding per claim
and cross-repo ambiguity refusal; say whether that is sufficient.
