## Read this before you interpret your own number

**The gold's disjoint slice is coarse, and that is a property of the gold rather than of
your method.** T2.57 shipped 12 lexically-disjoint positives, reading doc 17 §5.2's "at
least half lexically disjoint" as half of the *contradiction* pairs on the grounds that only
positives enter a recall measurement. Consequence: your miss rate on that slice has a
granularity of 1/12 ≈ 8.3%, so §5.2's recommended 20% writer-trigger bar is **breached at 3
misses, not 2**. The amendment logged today says explicitly that T2.52 must not report a
miss rate as if it were finer than the gold can express. State the granularity beside the
number.

records which slices exist, what each was designed to catch, and which labels rest on
ratified versus unratified grounds.
