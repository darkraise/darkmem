**Why these.** The brief prefers real repo markdown over invented prose
(doc 17 §6 rejects synthetic-only gold as unrepresentative) with the
measurement-content screen overriding that preference. These six are the
documents that survive the screen while spanning four genres — product prose,
contract rules, operator procedure, QA checklist — so the premise distribution
is not one register. All six are this project's own writing in an Apache-2.0
repo; **no third-party material was used**, so the licensing question the brief
raises does not arise.

**All `.md`, deliberately.** No `.txt` (the note parser's heuristics produce
untyped paragraph/list blocks with no heading levels — strictly less structure
than markdown gives, and the brief prefers markdown) and no `.pdf` (nothing
project-owned exists as PDF; converting a markdown doc to PDF to have one would
be manufactured provenance).

## How the measurement-content screen was run

The hazard: a committed premise later shown to a judge, and read by every pair
author, must not carry gate results, measured judge scores, or bar values.

1. **Excluded wholesale, unread**: `CLAUDE.md` (measured figures and live
   credentials), `docs/planning/**` including `gate-ledger.md`,
   `docs/design/**`, `docs/superpowers/specs/**`, `docs/eval/**` (the
   measurement artifacts themselves), `docs/2026-*` audit reports.
2. **Pattern screen over every remaining candidate**, with `rg -i`:
   `recall|precision|clopper|judge|qualification|\bbar\b|\bbars\b|gate ledger|
   G-[A-F]\b|F1|0\.9[05]|contradiction_|cleared|scored|corroborat`, then a
   second pass for `0\.[0-9]{2,}|sk-[A-Za-z0-9]|faithfulness|groundedness|
   ladder|false.promotion|promotion_|entail|span_gold|gold set|Clopper|upper
   bound`, then a third for `verif|badge|disputed|confirmed|candidate|gate|
   bench|eval|measure`. Every hit was read in context.
3. **Results.** The six selected documents: zero measurement hits. All `\bbar\b`
   hits are UI bar charts and progress bars; "verification points" is QA
   vocabulary; "cleared" is a session cookie; no decimal figure, no credential,
   no gate/judge/bar vocabulary anywhere in the pool.
4. **Rejected on the screen**, with the hit that rejected them:
   - `docs/two-door-parity.md` — names G-F, the faithfulness gate and the
     judge's missing qualification record. Gate-status content about the very
     gate this gold feeds; also self-referential (premises describing the gate
     that will consume them).
   - `docs/releases/v0.1-demonstration.md` — "because G-C, G-D, G-E and G-F
     are…" plus `verification_unmeasured`: gate-status content.
   - `docs/e2e/README.md` — conservative exclusion: two benign-looking lines
     ("what `scripts/gate.sh --full` invokes", "latency + throughput
     measurements" as a filename gloss) carry no figures, but the pool did not
     need it and a premise naming the merge gate is cheap to avoid.
   - `docs/e2e/ui-test-plan.md` — screens clean, excluded for composition, not
     contamination: same genre as `test-plan.md` + `checklist.md`, and adding
     918 more lines of checklist would tilt the distribution further toward one
     register while doubling the committed screening surface.

**Contamination disclosure (the brief requires stating it, not a clean-room
claim).** This selection was made by an agent whose context auto-injects
`CLAUDE.md`, which states measured judge scores (contradiction recall figures,
entailment precision/recall), bar values, and Clopper-Pearson bounds. That
exposure shaped nothing in the *premises* — the screen above is exactly what
keeps those figures out of the committed text, and none survive in the pool —
but whoever authors pairs should know the selector was not blind. The selector
authored no pair and no label.

## The arithmetic — target, slack, and why six documents

Stated before authoring, per the brief's instruction to derive rather than
guess:

- **Pair target: 260** = the 200 floor (doc 17 §6) + 30% headroom, so that
  retirement of up to 60 pairs during the two blind passes and owner
  adjudication cannot drag the gold under its floor and force a re-harvest.
  (The claim/fact gold retired pairs at every review round; 30% is slack for
  that, not a prediction.)
- **Independence rule: plan ~1 pair per anchor block, hard cap 2.** T2.124's
  finding — four pairs of one construction on one premise is "correlated
  evidence wearing four rows" — is read here as a planning constraint, not
  a rule for the owner's LABELLING.md.
- **Block demand ≈ 290.** 260 pairs at ~1 block each, plus the
  multi-span-composition slice consuming ≥ 2 blocks per pair (≈ 26 pairs if
  ten slices are roughly balanced → ≥ 26 extra blocks), ≈ 286, rounded up.
- **Supply: 800 blocks; 491 with ≥ 40 chars; 169 with ≥ 80 chars.**
  491 / 290 ≈ **1.7× slack** on anchorable blocks overall. The prose-hungry
  slices (span-truncation, paraphrase, weakening, overreach, value-swap —
  ≈ 130 pairs at balance) need substantial sentences: 169 blocks ≥ 80 chars
  gives ≈ 1.3× slack there, the tightest ratio in the plan and the reason the
  four prose documents are in the pool at all.
- **Why six documents and not two:** the two QA documents alone would meet the
  raw count (662 blocks) in a single register with only 23 paragraphs ≥ 80
  chars. The four prose documents contribute 54 of the pool's 77 long
  paragraphs and all of the connected-argument prose. Fewer documents met the
  count; six meet the distribution.

| `multi-span-composition` | 129 headings each scoping following blocks; runbook step sequences; setup-flow paragraphs split across blocks | authorable; any claim joining a heading's scope to its body, or two procedure steps, needs ≥ 2 blocks |
| `structure-fragment` | 560 list items, 7 table blocks, 129 heading lines | authorable; the densest material in the pool |
| transferable six | ordinary varied prose across all four prose docs plus test-plan narrative | authorable |
| temporal | correctly absent — no validity windows in block spans; the pool's dates are ordinary content | per spec, does not transfer |

**One structural fact authors must know:** the markdown parser runs the
`commonmark` preset, which has **no table extension** — a pipe table mints no
`table` block and is not skipped either; it survives as **one `paragraph` block
whose text is the header row, separator and data rows joined by newlines**
(verified in the manifest: readme's env-var table is a single 7-line paragraph
block). So every table-fragment pair is a *sub-block* `char_span` into that
paragraph — citing a data row without its header — never a cross-block
construction. The authoring brief's "you need … a table" is satisfied, but in
this form.

## Judgement calls, all of them

1. **Frozen copies, renamed flat.** The sources are copies, so the live docs
   keep evolving without breaking anchors; `--check` binds pairs to the copies.
   Names were flattened (`docs/e2e/checklist.md` → `e2e-checklist.md`) so the
   manifest `uri` carries the provenance without a directory tree.
2. **Conservative exclusions over pool size** — `docs/e2e/README.md` and
   `ui-test-plan.md`, reasons above.
3. **`.md`-only**, reasons above.
4. **Six docs, not fewer**, distribution argument above.
5. **The anaphora count (14) is a floor, not a census** — the regex was kept
   narrow to avoid overstating authorability; the slice needs ≈ 26 pairs and
   has three families to draw from.

## Findings against the brief and the tooling

1. **The brief's `.txt` claim is wrong as stated.** "A `.txt` note parses to
   one block containing the whole document" — verified false in general:
   `parse_note` splits on blank lines and typed a four-chunk note into
   2 `paragraph` + 2 `list` blocks. The one-block behaviour it describes is a
   property of the specific single-chunk fixtures that motivated the sentence,
   not of the parser. The conclusion it supports (prefer markdown) still holds,
   for the better reason that the note heuristics carry no heading levels or
   typed structure.
2. **`span_harvest.py`'s default `--database-url` is `darkmem_test`** (also the
   `SPAN_HARVEST_DATABASE_URL` fallback). That database belongs to pytest, and
   the module docstring's own usage example omits the flag — anyone following
   it writes harvest documents into pytest's database. This run passed an
   explicit scratch URL; the default is a footgun left unfixed here because the
   brief scopes this step to selection, harvest and record.
3. **Pipe tables mint no `table` block** (finding above, restated as a tooling
   fact): commonmark preset, so table material reaches the gold only as
   sub-block spans of a `paragraph` block. Not a defect — doc 16 §9's block
   vocabulary has no table type — but the authoring brief's phrasing implies a
   table block exists to cite.
