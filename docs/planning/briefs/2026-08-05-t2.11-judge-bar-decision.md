# Brief — Fable analysis: T2.11 is blocked by a judge that missed its bar

**Analysis and options only. Write no implementation, and do not re-run the measurement
hoping for a better number.** Runbook §7: "A gate number lands below its bar. That is the
gate working. Report the number; never re-run until it passes, and never widen the bar to
fit the result."

**Tasks:** T2.11, T2.26, T2.40, T2.41, T2.54 in `docs/planning/tasks/E2-truth-core.md`.
**Ledger:** `docs/planning/gate-ledger.md` — read its rules section first.

## The situation

T2.26 landed the `verification`-purpose judge. Its contradiction precision measured
**0.8182 against a 0.95 bar stated in writing beforehand**, so no qualification record was
minted and none exists on disk (`app/services/verification_qualification.json` absent).
`VerificationJudge.judge()` refuses unconditionally without one — verify that in
`backend/app/services/verification_judge.py`.

Consequences, which are the point: T2.11 (`contradiction_scan`) cannot ship, and it is a
`Depends` of **T2.40, the G-C evidence run**. G-C gates the code-deriver lane (T2.27,
T2.28, T2.48) and Phase-2 exit. So one measurement below its bar is currently holding a
quarter of the epic.

## What to do

Establish first whether the bar and the measurement are even comparable — read what T2.26
actually measured, on what gold, at what size, and whether the 0.95 was stated for that
metric on that population. A bar and a number that do not describe the same thing is a
different problem from a judge that is simply not good enough, and it has a different fix.

Then lay out the real options and their consequences. Among them, at minimum: improving the
judge (what would plausibly move contradiction precision, and is it prompt, model, gold, or
task definition); splitting the bar so contradiction detection and entailment qualify
separately, if they are separable claims; narrowing what the judge is *used for* so a
weaker judge is lawful for a narrower job; and re-scoping T2.11 to a deterministic
shortlist that needs no judge at all. Say what each costs and what it forecloses.

Note the interaction with **T2.54**, which is blocked only on the owner's ~3h labelling
sitting and which T2.41 (G-D) depends on. If a better gold set is part of any answer, say
whether that sitting is on the critical path for T2.11 too.

**Do not propose widening the 0.95.** If you conclude the bar itself was set wrong, say so
explicitly as a finding for the controller and give the reasoning — that is a ruling, not a
tuning, and it belongs in the ledger.
