## G1 — the scan misses the exact shape T2.28 will write

The rewritten scan quantifies over all 208 modules and catches its three declared emission
shapes. The reviewer ran **fifteen** shapes through it and it misses twelve. The one that
matters: **pydantic constructor keywords** — `SomeResponse(verification_status=row[...])` —
which is the standard idiom for a new route and is precisely the shape of T2.28's response
surface. Also missed: `.update()` keywords, and generic row copies (`dict(row)`,
`{**row}`, column-loop comprehensions).

**Widen `_emissions`.** The reviewer calls this a small, testable extension and it is the
right fix rather than a note in T2.28's brief — a blind spot recorded in a downstream
brief is a convention someone must remember, which is exactly what this module exists to
replace. Cover at minimum: constructor and call keywords, `dict.update` keywords, and the
generic-copy shapes. For each newly covered shape, plant it and show the build goes red;
say in your report which of the fifteen you cover and which you knowingly do not, because
a scan whose reach is unstated is one nobody can rely on.

## G2 — the status filter is an inversion oracle

Measured by the reviewer, pre-existing, and missed by the original attack: with the gate
closed, `?verification_status=corroborated` on the facts list returns **exactly the
suppressed fact**, and `?verification_status=candidate` returns nothing. Membership
recovers the withheld rung per fact.

The strict M1 sentence survives — the label itself is never served — but the harm the gate
exists to prevent does not care about that distinction. An agent that can enumerate which
facts are really `corroborated` has the promoted claim, whatever the response body says.
And T2.28 is the task that would fill that filter with born-`corroborated` rows, which is
why this closes now rather than being triaged later.

**Fix: the filter keys on the served status, the same value the response carries.** A
filter and a field that disagree about the same row are two answers to one question. Drive
it at the door with the gate closed and with a minted record, and assert both directions —
the suppressed fact must not be reachable by filtering for the withheld rung, and must be
reachable by filtering for what it actually serves.
