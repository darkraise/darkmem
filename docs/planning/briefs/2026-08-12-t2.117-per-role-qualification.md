## What you are building

`VerificationJudge.judge()` is one gate over all four `BAR_METRICS` serving two consumers
that ask different questions. Split the **record** by consumer role, keeping **one judge
identity**. The entailment role gates on `entailed_precision` / `entailed_recall`; the
contradiction role gates on the contradiction pair and **stays unqualified**.

## The amendment must carry all of this, or it is not the amendment that was ratified

- **The grounds, citing no measured contradiction number**: doc 20 §4's registry already
  defines G-D as the entailment discrimination; the contradiction pair entered via one
  inheritance sentence at `17-test-and-eval-harness.md:147`; the consumer pairing is
  code-forced in both directions.
- **§6's rejected-alternatives clause named and distinguished** (`:161`): it rejects
  per-consumer *judges* — three purposes, three models — not per-role *records* over one
  identity. But roles do now hold qualification state independently, which is that
  clause's nearest neighbour, and an amendment silent on it is not honest.
- **The severed coupling, stated as a cost.** Under one bundled record, "articles may
  ship" implied "a qualified contradiction judge exists". After the split it does not.
  Defensible on doc 08 §5.3's asymmetry, but it is a real weakening.
- **The removed leak bound.** Recall ≥ 0.95 currently bounds contradicted→entailed misses
  — the ones that corrupt coverage — at 5%. After the split only entailed precision prices
  them, at the pooled base rate. Record that any minted entailment record must disclose the
  contradicted→entailed confusion cell.
- **The 2026-08-08 lineage.** R1 is the *second* post-failure re-cut of G-D. Own it: the
  pattern is survivable only because in both cases the failed bar never moved and the
  failed capability never became qualified.
- **The expiry check**: if the contradiction role is never re-attempted on either
  sanctioned move within roughly two quarters, the split reads retroactively as burial.

## Hard constraints

- **Symmetric.** Each role gated on the discrimination its consumer actually makes.
- **Bars unchanged**: `contradiction_recall_bar` 0.95, `contradiction_precision_bar` 0.70.
  If you find yourself editing a bar value, stop — that is the opposite of this task.
- **Mint nothing.** The entailment role's record comes from `T2.125` on a fresh gold
  (`T2.124`). Q4 refused minting from the T2.114 artifact, so an offline re-score path is
  explicitly *not* wanted here.
- **Pin the tripwires as tests, not prose**: a contradiction-role bar relaxation, and any
  qualification contact with `pairs_sha256 4975c5cc` for **any** role, must fail the build.
- **Scan-enforced pin** that `claim_entailment` consumes only the entailed discrimination
  and `contradiction_scan` only the contradicted one — machine-check the derivation's
  premise the way the second-RRF-constant scan does.
- The Tier-C scan must keep dead-ending exactly as it does today.
- Make no live model call; touch no gold; do not run `./scripts/gate.sh`.
