## Why the gold is being grown

Not for headroom — that reasoning is wrong and repeating it would misdirect the work.
Three measured runs failed on an error *rate*; a larger gold at the same rate fails
identically. The reason is **contamination**: the current gold carries three recorded
`gold_touching_attempts`, and its failure census is public. A judge measured against pairs
whose failure modes are written down is being measured on material fitted to it.

So the property you are producing is **an uncontaminated measurement surface**, and the
only thing that makes it uncontaminated is that its author could not aim.

## What to produce

Additional `claim-fact` pairs, merged into `entailment/gold.json` under the existing
schema, holding every property the loader enforces:

- doc 17 §11 composition: **≥ 50% `l2-fact` premises**, each anchored to a committed
  `l2_harvest.json` manifest row (premise byte-equal to the minted fact, full `fact_key`).
  The loader refuses any other `l2-fact` shape — that refusal is the anti-forgery property,
  not an obstacle to route around.
- Verdict/slice lawfulness: the loader rejects e.g. a `verbatim` contradiction. Read
  `SLICES` and the verdict compatibility rules rather than inferring them.
- `declared_verdict_counts`, `declared_slice_counts` and `declared_provenance_counts`
  **recomputed from the pairs**, never hand-maintained.
- `label_source` honest: `adjudicated` means two blind passes agreed, `owner` means a human
  ruled. Do not stamp either without the thing it names having happened.

**Sizing.** The binding consideration is granularity on the contradiction axis, and it is
the reason this task exists rather than a detail: `contradiction_recall_bar` is 0.95, and
recall resolves in steps of 1/N where N is the **gold-contradicted census**. At the
current 55, one miss moves recall 0.018 and 0.95 is not distinguishable from 0.945. Size
the contradicted census so 0.95 is a bar a measurement can actually clear or miss, and
state the arithmetic in your report. Growing the other verdicts proportionally keeps the
set balanced, but the contradicted census is what the bar needs.

## After the pairs exist

- **Re-draw the blind resample.** The draw pins `gold_sha256`; growth moves it, so the
  recorded resample does not carry over. `resample_draw.py` owns this.
- The grown gold must pass `judge_qualification.gate_eligibility` on its own terms — it
  will report ineligible until a fresh resample is scored, and that is correct.
- `gold_touching_attempts` resets with the gold file. Say so in the report.

## Do not

Run `run_judge_qualification.py` in any mode. Measuring is not this task, and a judge
contact against the new gold before it is resampled spends the very freshness you are
creating. Mint no qualification record.

## Verify

`./scripts/gate.sh` green before you finish. Foreground tests with an explicit `timeout`
**and** the Bash tool's own timeout parameter. `git branch --show-current` before every
commit. No stray processes; `darkmem_bench_t<sha10>` is lawful and must not be dropped
(runbook §8).

## Report

State the composition arithmetic, the contradicted-census sizing and its granularity
consequence, what you read and what you did not, and — plainly — anything you saw that you
were asked not to. An author who accidentally reads the profile has not failed; an author
who reads it and stays quiet has.
