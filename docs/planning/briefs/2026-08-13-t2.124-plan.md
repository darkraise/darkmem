## Why this task exists, and the one rule its justification must obey

G-D's judge-side critical path. Doc 17 §6 (amended 2026-08-12, T2.117) splits
qualification into one record per consumer **role** over one judge identity:
`entailment` for the article claim pass, `contradiction` for the Tier-C scan. Both
records are absent. The contradiction role **missed its bar and stays missed** —
0.7937 (deepseek-v4-flash) and 0.8814 / 0.9020 (gpt-5.5) against 0.95 — and that is
a measured fact this task does not touch. The entailment role passed both bars on
both judges, and the owner nevertheless **refused to mint it** from T2.114's spent
artifact. So the entailment role qualifies on a fresh gold or not at all.

**The justification for retiring pair set `4975c5cc` must cite no measured result.**
That constraint is the point: a gold retired because a judge scored badly on it is a
gold retired to get a better number. Two independent grounds are available that
name no score, both already established:

1. **Publication contamination.** That gold's false-positive censuses, its duplicate
   findings and its per-slice miss decompositions are all published in
   `gate-ledger.md`. The set is unusable for any future qualification for any role,
   independently of how anything scored on it.
2. **The entailed class was never sized to its own bar.** T2.118 found no
   T2.110-style lattice sizing was ever run for it — pooled entailed enforces
   0.9017, so the 0.90 bar is not expressible — and it carries five duplicated
   propositions over seven findings including one three-row component.

Write the plan so that ground 1 alone would suffice. If any step's rationale needs a
score to make sense, that step is wrong.

## What the plan must decide, step by step

**1. The sampling frame, stated before any pair exists.** Where premises and claims
come from, how many, and the rule that selects them. It must not reference which
slices any judge has missed — that is the contamination channel the whole task
exists to close, and T2.115's amendment log records a near-miss where a variant was
chosen by a contaminated controller.

**2. Lattice sizing for BOTH classes.** A bar is only meaningful if it is exactly
representable on the census it gates. With N pairs in a class, achievable scores are
k/N, so a 0.95 bar needs N ≡ 0 (mod 20) and a 0.90 bar needs N ≡ 0 (mod 10);
otherwise the effective bar is the next representable value above it — a bar nobody
argued for. Size the **entailed** class (gated at precision ≥ 0.95, recall ≥ 0.90)
and the **contradicted** class (precision ≥ 0.70, recall ≥ 0.95) so every bar that
gates them lands exactly on the lattice. State the arithmetic. T2.115's derivation
that 0.95 cannot be exact on two populations simultaneously (`N ≡ 0` and
`55 + N ≡ 0 (mod 20)` has no solution) is the shape of the argument required —
check whether the analogous obstruction bites here and say so either way.

**3. Independence, computed both ways for both classes.** The census-independence
disclosure must be produced for the contradicted *and* the entailed class, on the
rows reading and the independent-propositions reading, using T2.123's connected-
component counting rather than the finding count it replaced. Say at which step this
is computed and what happens if a class comes back with components wider than two.

**4. Authoring party ≠ adjudicating party.** Name the separation concretely: who
authors, who adjudicates, and what mechanically prevents one agent doing both. T2.54
established that a Claude-family second pass adds near-zero bits and diverges
identically, which is why the n=93 resample was deliberately not delegated — the
plan must not reintroduce that failure under a new name.

**5. Retiring `4975c5cc`.** Recorded retired from qualification use, with the
refusal pinned in the runner for **every** role. `refuse_retired_pair_set` already
exists — confirm it covers all roles and both modes, and say what test pins it.

**6. The archive sequence and the floor exemption.** The archive commit moves
`entailment/gold.json` to `entailment/retired/gold-4975c5cc.json` and deliberately
leaves no gold at the canonical path, so gold-consuming benchlib tests are expected
red from that commit until the completed census lands. The exemption is already
claimed in the task's own text and covers exactly that red. The plan's closing step
must re-assert the whole floor (`./scripts/gate.sh`) for the sequence. Any other
failure during the sequence is a finding, not part of the exemption.

**7. Cost and stopping points.** How many pairs, roughly how long, and where the
owner should be asked rather than told. Say plainly if the honest answer is that
this cannot be completed in one sitting.

## What would make this plan wrong

- A step whose justification cites a measured score.
- A sizing that makes a bar expressible by moving the bar rather than the census.
- An authoring/adjudicating split that is a label rather than a mechanism.
- A sequence in which the gold is unusable for longer than the archive→land window
  the exemption covers.
- Silence about a step you could not decide. An open question named in the plan is
  worth more than a decision made to look complete.
