# T2.31 prerequisite — wire the heat and recency signals

Not T2.31 itself. This lands the half T2.31's Verify line assumes already exists, and
records why the measurement still cannot be run.

## Why this was needed

T2.31 reads as a measure-and-decide task: run the ablation, record the delta, activate or
leave at zero. I ran it and got an **exact zero delta on every slice**. That was the
arithmetic, not a finding — `ProcessingPipeline._apply_rank_fusion_factor` fed only
`confidence` and `importance` into the signal dict, and its own docstring said the
heat/recency arms "have no signal source wired yet (T2.31) and contribute exactly 1.0".
Both legs were the same ranking by construction, at any weight.

T2.29 landed the substrate (`memory_access_stats`, `access_count` + `last_accessed`) and
deliberately derived nothing from it. This is the derivation and the wiring.

## What ships

- **`rank_fusion.heat_signal` / `recency_signal`** — the derivations, beside the arm that
  spends them rather than beside the counter, so a second consumer cannot invent a second
  scale for one signal. Heat **saturates** (`1 - e^(-count/HEAT_SCALE)`) rather than
  normalising: normalising by the corpus or page maximum makes one row's signal depend on
  which other rows came back, so the same memory scores differently in two result sets and
  the doors stop being comparable. Recency is exponential decay on `last_accessed` with a
  30-day half-life — **access** recency, since that is the column T2.29 offers and
  `updated_at` means "content changed".
- **`access_arms_active`** — the gate, so an install with both arms at zero pays no
  `memory_access_stats` read at all. Inert also means free.
- **`HybridSearchService._attach_access_signals`** — stamps the signals on the fused rows
  in the **shared** channel. This is the load-bearing placement: the chat door reads arm
  signals off the row and the search door re-reads `memories`, so deriving separately at
  each would agree exactly while both arms sat at zero and diverge the moment either was
  flipped on. That is the T2.58 divergence shape arriving through the signals instead of
  the channel.
- Absence stays absence: a memory with no counter row gets **no keys**, and `arm_factor`
  treats an absent signal as exactly 1.0. Writing a 0.0 instead would demote every newly
  captured memory for as long as the arm is on — the opposite of what a heat prior is for.

Four mutation drills, each caught by a different test.

## The measurement is still blocked, and the wiring is what exposed it

**The ablation harness cannot validly measure `w_heat`, and my own change is what makes
that true.** `arm_ablation` resets the bench database once, then runs the off leg and the
on leg over the same corpus with no reset between them. Every search — including every one
of the eval's 102 gold queries — bumps the counters through
`HybridSearchService._record_access`.

So the on leg's heat is **entirely manufactured by the off leg's traversal of the gold
set**. The memories carrying heat are disproportionately the ones the off leg correctly
surfaced, which means the arm would be scored on a signal derived from the answer key. A
win measured this way is self-fulfilling, and recording it would put exactly the kind of
hollow number in the arm-activation log that the ledger exists to keep out.

Before my change this was harmless: nothing read the table, so leg-order contamination
could not reach a score.

**Two resolutions, and choosing between them is T2.31's own measurement-design call
(Fable tier, runbook §2) rather than a mechanical fix:**

1. **Seed and freeze** — establish a realistic access history before both legs and
   suppress recording during measurement. Requires deciding what "realistic" is, which is
   a modelling decision that belongs in the gold protocol, not in a runner.
2. **Reset between legs** — clean, but leaves the on leg with zero heat, so the arm is
   inert again and the measurement is vacuous.

Neither is a snap decision, and the harness protocol is frozen (doc 17 §7 / `FROZEN.md`),
so this brief stops here rather than editing it unilaterally.
