## What and why

T2.52 is specified to measure the contradiction candidate generator's recall "against
contradiction-pair gold". **Nothing built that gold**, so T2.52 is unexecutable and T2.33
sits behind it. You are building the gold, not the generator and not the scorer.

## What the gold must encode, because the veto is absolute

Doc 08 §4.2 makes one non-echo contradiction over an overlapping validity window an
**absolute veto** — not a vote. That makes a false positive in this gold far more expensive
than a false negative: a mislabelled pair teaches the generator to demote a true fact to
`disputed`, and consensus cannot outvote it. Weight your labelling effort accordingly and
say so in the gold's own documentation.

The three hard negative classes are the point of the set, and each exists because it is a
case a naive lexical generator gets wrong:

- **Non-overlapping windows.** "postgres in 2024" and "mysql in 2025" are a *succession*
  the belief axis already models, not a contradiction.
- **Echoes.** The same origin restating itself is not corroboration and not contradiction;
  `origin_key` is the identity that decides this.
- **Lexically similar but unrelated.** High surface similarity, no opposition.
