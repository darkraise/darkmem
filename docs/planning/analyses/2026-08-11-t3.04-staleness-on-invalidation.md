# Tier-3 escalation — doc 09 §4.4 contradicts itself on invalidation

**Raised 2026-08-11 while executing T3.04. Owner ruling needed.**
Runbook §5 Tier-3: "a design doc is wrong or ambiguous … do not proceed."

## What T3.04 asks for, and what is blocked

T3.04's done-when has three clauses. Two are delivered and tested
(`29ecb501`): the FK is enforced, and an invalidated fact leaves its citation
intact. The third — **"and the article marked stale"** — cannot be delivered
without choosing between two halves of doc 09 §4.4 that do not agree.

## The contradiction, in §4.4's own words

§4.4 states the gate and then enumerates its triggers:

> the gate is `fact_set_hash` over the subject's **non-disputed
> corroborated/confirmed facts only**: an article goes stale exactly when the
> *knowledge* it was built from changed (a fact got corroborated, disputed,
> **invalidated**, or a new one crossed the bar)

Three of those four triggers move a fact in or out of "non-disputed
corroborated/confirmed", so the stated predicate delivers them:

| Trigger | Moves the set? |
|---|---|
| a fact got corroborated | yes — it joins |
| a fact got disputed | yes — it leaves |
| a new one crossed the bar | yes — it joins |
| **a fact got invalidated** | **no** |

Invalidation sets `invalid_at`. It does **not** change `verification_status`
and does **not** set `expired_at` — verified in the tree: two live writers
(`KnowledgeTripleRepository.close_window` and `verification_recompute`'s
stated-expiry arm) set `invalid_at` alone, and the latter's docstring says so
in terms ("`invalid_at`, not `expired_at`, and the difference is the whole
ruling"). So an invalidated fact remains non-disputed, corroborated and
unexpired, the hash does not move, and the article is never labelled stale.

**§4.4's enumeration names a trigger §4.4's own predicate cannot fire.**

## Why this is not the loop's call

Both readings are defensible and they are not equivalent:

1. **The predicate is right and the enumeration is loose.** Staleness tracks
   the *verified* set, and an invalidated fact is still verified — its belief
   window closed, which is a different axis. Doc 04 keeps those axes separate
   deliberately. Under this reading `invalidated` should not be in the list.
2. **The enumeration is right and the predicate is under-specified.** An
   invalidated fact is no longer *believed*, so it is no longer a supporting
   fact, and the hash should carry the repo's canonical
   `OPEN_BELIEF_PREDICATE` (`expired_at IS NULL AND invalid_at IS NULL`) —
   which already exists and is what `fact_sources` counts corroboration on.

Reading 2 is the one I would pick, and that is exactly why I am not picking it:
it **widens** what counts as a knowledge change, so it makes articles go stale
more often and drives more re-synthesis — a cost, and a change to semantics
T3.02 already froze into `knowledge_articles.fact_set_hash`. Choosing it
silently would also make the L3 staleness basis diverge from §4.2's *gather*
predicate, which doc 09 states without any belief-window clause.

## Why it is being raised now rather than at T3.05

T3.05 owns the staleness gate, so this looks like its problem. It is being
raised here because **T3.04's done-when depends on the answer**, and because
the scoped re-review of T3.03 flagged the same tension with the note that it is
"worth recording before the hash column's semantics are frozen by
implementation". They are frozen now — T3.02 ships the column and T3.03 reads
it — so the next task to touch it inherits the choice rather than making it.

## What is needed

A ruling on which half of §4.4 is authoritative, and an amendment to the other.
If reading 2 wins, T3.05 changes the hash basis to `OPEN_BELIEF_PREDICATE` and
T3.04's third clause follows from it with no further work; if reading 1 wins,
`invalidated` comes out of §4.4's enumeration and T3.04's third clause is
struck as unachievable-by-design.

**T3.04 is marked `blocked` on this clause, with its other two clauses done and
tested.**
