Every capture below was taken after the last UI change that affects it. The last change overall is e059e08b, the focus ring; it alters only the focused state, and every capture of a focused canvas (`ux-8-*`, the demo walk) was retaken after it. These sets exist at **both viewports in both themes**:

- the real-host walk (`ui-6-real-*`, `ux-6-real-*`, `ux-7-real-*`);

The `ux-4-*` twinkle pairs are 1280x800 dark only.

Fixed during this walkthrough, before stamping:
- **3ba9f56d, e3e9074b:** the dark core and satellite clumps clipped to white at density; satellites are held above the halo.
- **6ae3f2e3:** edges were drawn far too strong. Edge colour was blended as if premultiplied, so rest edges at 8% and 12% alpha landed near full strength, or as white lines in light, and dimmed edges never faded.
- **d92a4589:** the WebGL-loss state now uses the EmptyState wrapper.
- **818d7e9a:** the camera targeted a node's position from before the core settled.
- **1cae150d:** an in-flight camera animation refreshed a killed sigma after a rebuild.
- **f8fa758b:** a `?file=` link selected nothing, so the camera never moved.
- **e059e08b:** the canvas's focus ring was clipped to one edge. It is now drawn on top, inset.

Storybook's preview renders a "Not Found" line at top left of every story. That is the preview's own router, which predates this branch (ledger, Task 8 discovered). It is not part of the built UI: the demo-host captures have no such line.
