Both directories are git-ignored and stay local.

**The live page reflects the deployed build, not this branch.** Nothing was deployed in this run. Its dark captures `.playwright-mcp/constellation-real-data/live-default-*-dark.png` show the old arrangement: zoomed in, with labels on most nodes. The live captures contain real memory text, so they stay local and are not quoted here.

- **Selected:** every white pixel lies inside the adjacent labels' text ("Memory 726" at 1280; "Memory 127", "Memory 350" and "Memory 726" at 1920), by bounding box. That is label ink, not glow.
- **EntityLayer:** the white pixels sit at glow centres under entity diamonds, both in the core and out among the clumps; the enlarged crop shows a white-centred cyan glow and white-washed outlines inside it. The diamonds draw through `NodeDiamondProgram` with ordinary premultiplied blending, not the additive glow pass, so the white comes from the glow underneath. Spec Architecture §7 promises "no part of the canvas sums to white at the fit view", and the entity layer is a state the contract's UX-5 matrix names.
- **Inference, not verified:** with the entity layer on, `classifyWithCut` pushes each core- or satellite-role entity into `classification.core` or its clump (the A4 output counts 1,100 core-role entities on the 1,000 board). The renderer's `trackDensityGain` call filters entities out of the halo only. The core and satellite gain are therefore computed over a set that includes 3-unit diamonds that the glow pass never draws.
- **What holds:** the core ≥ satellite ≥ halo brightness order, and zooming in restores full size and brightness (`ui-5-zoomed-*`). On All, the core centre reads pale and washed-out pink, with no pixel reaching 250.
