## eg-0450 is NOT simply a fourth instance of the sortal defect

A shape-free scan flags four entailed pairs asserting an unestablished kind —
eg-0425, eg-0450, eg-0508, eg-0651, all "machine"/"service" riding
attributively inside a definite naming an entity ("the data-06 **machine**").
Both labellers ruled eg-0450 `unsupported`. **But pass B's argument cuts the
other way and is not obviously wrong:**

> there is no existential here; the referent is fixed by the *name*, and the
> sortal is descriptive content in a **referring expression**, which
> presupposition-wise patterns with the exhaustivity rule's *protected* class.

So two ratified rules pull in opposite directions:

- the **Frame rule** says "a definite article does not shelter it" — but argues
  it from the restrictor of an *asserted existential*;
- the **exhaustivity rule** protects *presupposed* content, and a sortal inside
  a referring definite is presupposed, not asserted.

**This is a ruling, not a repair.** Treating the four as defects would be the
controller making a rule call that two ratified rules disagree about. They are
left untouched.

## Constructions both labellers flagged as unplaced

- **"interacts with"**, **"calls on"** — generic engagement verbs over `USES`.
  The dependency-strength rule places necessity vocabulary and nothing else.
- **"for the duration of"** — between the window rule's "until" and the
  implicature rule's "during".
- **instance-hood** — the exclusive-slot host rule keys on "a single named
  instance" and states no test; the only guidance is an authoring-side skip log
  resting on harvest evidence a blind labeller may not use.
- **"storage backend" / "datastore"** — compound role-sortals between the
  earned "resource" and the partitioning "database".
- **"built from"** — ruled inside `HAS_PART` today, flagged again as a judgment
  call.

## The pattern worth naming

Every blind round today has surfaced 3–6 new gaps, and the count is not
falling. The gold's pairs were authored against a rulebook that does not decide
the constructions they use — so each round's labellers reach for a rule, find
none, apply a test, and flag it. That is the rulebook being underspecified
relative to its own corpus, not a run of bad pairs.
