# Pre-registration of the Layer-3 redraw — written BEFORE the sitting

Committed before `resample_draw` is run, so nothing below can be adjusted to
fit a result.

## What will be sat

`glm-5.2` (owner ruling: a third model, on a z.ai key), n = 93, bar **≤ 1
disagreement**, seeded from the repaired gold's own sha. This is the task's
**last permitted failure** — two consecutive stop T2.124.

## What is known to be risky, stated up front

1. **`eg-0529` is guaranteed to be in the draw.** It is `contradicted`, and the
   contradicted stratum is a full census (80 of 80). One blind labeller read it
   against the recorded label before the owner ruled the instance-hood default
   (2a) in the gold's favour. If the sitting model reads it as that labeller
   did, it consumes the entire budget alone.
2. **`glm-5.2` has no measurement on this gold or on any of its constructions.**
   Fable's 9–34% estimate was computed from `deepseek-v4-pro`'s ~46% rejection
   rate on the bare-sortal population and **does not transfer**. We are
   trading a known risk for an unmeasured one, deliberately, on the owner's
   ruling.
3. **v4-pro rejected 11 of the 25 probe-measured pairs that are still live** —
   but every one of those rejections was made against a rulebook that did not
   contain the bare-sortal clause. The clause now states that "component" and
   "system" are vacuous, which is what the gold's labels assume. **Verified: the
   resample's `rulebook_excerpt` carries all eight of today's clauses**, so the
   sitting model is given the rules those rejections lacked. Whether that closes
   the gap is the substantive thing this sitting measures.

## What will NOT happen, whatever the number says

- No re-run to improve it, no second model, no redraw on the same gold.
- No bar moved, in either direction.
- Nothing written into `gold.json` unless it clears.
- A miss is recorded as a miss and reported, and it is the second of two.

## What a clear result would and would not license

A pass says an independent third-vendor instrument, given the written rules,
reproduces this gold's labels at n = 93. It does **not** say the labels are
correct: the pairs were authored and adjudicated by Claude-family agents, and
the resample measures inter-model agreement more than label truth — the
runner's own docstring says so and it remains the honest caveat.
